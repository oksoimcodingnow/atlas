What you're looking at. Each grey dot is a portfolio: some % in A, the rest in B. As you slide from all-B to all-A, the dot traces a curve. The green point is the minimum-variance portfolio — the single mix with the least possible risk. Notice: when ρ is high (near +1) the curve is almost a straight line — mixing barely helps. As you drag ρ down, the curve bows left: you can get the same return for less risk than either asset alone. That leftward bow is the entire point of diversification.
