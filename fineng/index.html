Financial Engineering · Year 3 · interactive study

The math, made playable.

Every course this semester, turned into something you can drag, tweak, and watch — because you understand a formula best once you've built the thing that runs it.

One studio per course. Pick a topic, move the sliders, see the idea move. Built on the same finance references that back the rest of Atlas, so the math is the math your exams use.

Why build instead of just read?

You can't fake an efficient frontier. Either the variance formula is right and the curve bends, or it isn't and the curve is wrong. Building each tool is the cheapest way to find out whether you actually understand the material — long before the exam does it for you.
