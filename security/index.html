Security, from the ground up.

How attacks actually work, and how defenders stop them — every major domain of cybersecurity, each as a lesson you can finish in a sitting.

Built like the Quant Academy: every lesson defines its terms, gives you something to play with, and ends with the one idea that sticks. No memorizing acronyms — understanding.

🛡️
The white-hat rule. You learn how attacks work so you can defend against them — that is what every security professional does. Everything here is for your own systems, intentionally-vulnerable practice labs, or authorized testing with written permission. Using these ideas against systems you don't own is a crime, and it's the opposite of the craft. Defenders understand offense; they don't commit it.

Where this path leads

Finish all fourteen and you'll have the working mental model behind the CompTIA Security+ exam and the foundation under CISSP and OSCP — not as memorized trivia, but as ideas you can reason from. More importantly, you'll read a headline about a breach and actually understand what happened, and you'll write your own code more safely because you've seen how the unsafe version gets broken.
