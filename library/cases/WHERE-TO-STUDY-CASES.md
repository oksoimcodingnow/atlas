# 📖 Where to Study Cases — Practice Sources

> Researched 24 Jun 2026. Places to find real case booklets + free case studies to practice on,
> for case-competition prep (Wonder Lab and beyond).

## 🥇 Start here — Thai / competition-specific (closest to your world)
- **TUBC — Thammasat Undergraduate Business Challenge** — Thailand's flagship, 28 yrs. Has a
  **downloadable Case Library** of past competition booklets to practice on.
  → Case Library: https://tubc.page/caseexample.html · Main: https://tubc.page/
- **Case Study Club** — write-ups of real competitions incl. TUBC; good to see *how winners think.*
  → https://casestudyclub.org/
- **Thammasat BBA case archive** — http://www.bba.bus.tu.ac.th/catcase.php

## 🌍 Free, world-class case studies (great for practice + "as seen at" ammo)
- **Yale SOM — Marketing Strategy Case Studies** (free) — Chipotle, Dove, Peloton, **TOMS**, SAP, etc.
  Directly useful: TOMS/Dove are precedents for our DHL pitch. → https://ocs.yale.edu/resources/marketing-strategy-case-studies/
- **MIT OpenCourseWare — Marketing Management cases** (free) → https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-810-marketing-management-fall-2010/pages/case-studies/
- **The Case Centre — free cases** (Harvard/Stanford/MIT collections) → https://www.thecasecentre.org (search "free cases")
- **Harvard Business Publishing — Educator Access** (free account → 70,000+ materials) → https://hbsp.harvard.edu/cases/
- **Oxford Bodleian LibGuide — Business Cases** (a map of where cases live) → https://libguides.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/business/business-cases

## 🎯 How to use them (practice loop)
1. **Read a TUBC booklet cold** → spend 15 min locking the ONE key question (don't rush this).
2. Run our own framework: **Situation (5C/SWOT) → Insight → Big Idea → Feasibility/ROI → Pitch.**
3. **Make a call and defend it** — judges grade reasoning + delivery, not "the right answer."
4. Compare your thinking to a **Case Study Club** write-up of the same/similar comp.
5. Save each practice case as its own master doc in this library (like `DHL.md`).

> Tip: the Yale free set is the best *quality-per-effort* — short, famous brands, marketing-focused,
> and several (TOMS, Dove) double as precedents you can cite in real pitches.
