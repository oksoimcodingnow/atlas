"X kg CO₂ ≈ Y km not driven" — here's the math.
DHL

1 kg CO₂ ≈ ~6 km  ·  10 kg CO₂ ≈ ~59 km not driven 🚗

✅ Why a comparison: makes an invisible gram tangible without overclaiming.
