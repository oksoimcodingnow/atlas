## 1. THE BRIEF & KEY QUESTION
**Problem:** DHL ServicePoint customers are very satisfied (**83%**) — but only about *service*, not
sustainability. DHL's real green work is **invisible at the moment of shipping.** Awareness is low.

> **Key Question (booklet p16):** How do we make customers realize that every time they ship abroad at
> one of DHL's **36 ServicePoints**, they're helping reduce environmental impact alongside DHL?

It's an **awareness/communication** problem — the green already exists; people just don't know.
**Constraints (p14):** ฿5,000,000 · 36 ServicePoints · 3 months · **no celebrities.**
**Judging (20% each):** Creativity · Insight · Feasibility · Impact/ROI (40% combined) · Storytelling.

## 2. SITUATION ANALYSIS (read first with the team)

**One line:** *DHL already does the green work and customers already love DHL — they just don't know the
two are connected. Connect them at the moment of shipping, compliantly, and let the customers who ship
most carry the message.*

**5C:** Company = market leader, real first-mover green creds, has done the work but never told it at the
counter · Customers = come for speed not green; SMEs highest value (repeat + own audience) · Competitors =
rivals also green but communicate poorly; the real rival is *indifference at the counter* · Collaborators
= SME sellers as broadcasters, staff, seller communities, owned channels (LINE/FB/IG/screens) · Context =
tightening green-marketing law + rising eco-awareness + green skepticism.

**Problem tree:** symptom (low awareness) → trunk (green is invisible at point of sale) → roots (no green
message on receipt/screen/staff; GoGreen Plus not surfaced to walk-ins; customers come for speed; legal
caution → under-communication).

**SWOT:** *S* — provable creds, a customer-usable product (GoGreen Plus), 83% warm audience, owned
touchpoints · *W* — story untold at point of sale, green isn't why they come · *O* — turn satisfaction
into pride, SME multiplier (free reach), specificity-as-trust · *T* — greenwashing/legal misstep, green
skepticism, attention scarcity, ฿5M/3mo limits.

**So what:** a winning idea must live at the **point of sale**, make the customer a **participant**, use a
**specific provable number in a human moment**, exploit the **SME multiplier**, fit the constraints, and
visibly respect **Green Claims**. → That's exactly what our system does (it's the situation's conclusion).

## 4. THE BIG IDEA + THE SYSTEM

### Big Idea: "Proof That Travels" — campaign line **"Ship Love. Ship Light."** (ส่งความรัก ส่งอย่างรักษ์โลก)
We don't *tell* people DHL is green — we hand them **proof they personally helped**, and let it travel:
onto the box, across the border, onto a national map, into the seller's shop.

### The 4 connected pieces — **Feel → Carry → Belong → Spread**
| Step | Piece | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| ① **Feel** | **Your Green Receipt** | "you funded a verified cut of X kg CO₂ ≈ Y km not driven" + QR (book-and-claim correct, GLEC method) |
| ② **Carry** | **Green Stamp** on the box | receiver *abroad* sees it → free cross-border reach |
| ③ **Belong** | **Thailand's Green Map** | localize DHL's real GoGreen Dashboard → collective national total |
| ④ **Spread** | **Seller Badge** | SMEs broadcast "Ships greener with DHL" → mirrors UOB's SME mission |

They plug together: the Receipt's data feeds the Map; the Stamp carries it abroad; the Badge makes sellers spread it.

### The loop-closer: **Green Shipper tier** (connective tissue, not a 5th piece)
Status earned by **repeat green choices** (not raw spend): 🌱 Sprout → 🥉 Bronze → 🥈 Silver → 🥇 Gold.
The Receipt's running total = the tier progress bar; the Seller Badge levels up with the tier. This is what
makes the loop *repeat* (fixes the awareness→habit gap). Lives in LINE OA (near-zero build).
*Guardrails:* reward choices not spend · keep it a 10-sec pitch beat · behaviour-named (Green-Claims safe) · don't paywall the good feeling.

## 7. CARBON / SAF SCIENCE (defend it under fire)
- SAF = jet fuel from renewable/waste sources, **drop-in** (same planes, **same speed** — kills "green = slower").
- Costs **2–4×** fossil jet fuel; **under 1%** of global jet fuel supply → *that scarcity is why book-and-claim exists.*
- Cuts **up to ~80% lifecycle CO₂ on waste feedstocks** (crop ~55%) — claim the certified figure, never a blanket 80%.
- **The lifecycle truth:** SAF emits ~same CO₂ at the tailpipe; the saving is the carbon *came from today's
  air* (biogenic), not ancient oil. "Fossil = one-way street; SAF = near-closed loop. Leftover ~20–50% is the energy to make it."

---

## 8. GREEN-CLAIMS & METHODOLOGY (the winner-separator)
- **Book-and-claim:** a customer's parcel may NOT physically fly on SAF — DHL buys SAF somewhere, certifies
  the reduction. → Say **"you funded a verified reduction via SAF,"** NEVER "your parcel flew on SAF."
- **Cite GLEC Framework / ISO 14083** — the universal logistics-emissions standard, foundation of ISO 14083,
  **and DHL is a GLEC member.** Citing it = citing the standard DHL itself uses. Unbeatable credibility.
- **EmpCo** binding **27 Sep 2026** — bans generic claims + offset "carbon neutral." DHL's insetting survives it.
- **Never say:** "green/eco-friendly/sustainable" alone, "emission-free," "climate neutral/positive."
- **Always:** specific %, explain *how* (GLEC/SAF/insetting), provable. *If you can't prove it, don't say it.*
- Concrete example to show (label "illustrative" unless from MyDHL+): *"SAF 8% → cut ~340g → ≈ 3 km not driven (GLEC/ISO 14083)."*

## 16. Q&A PREP — the devil's-advocate questions (have these ready)

1. **"A receipt just gets thrown away."** → It's also digital (LINE/email), on the branch screen, and in the staff line; the paper is one of *several* touchpoints, and the tier total persists.
2. **"Isn't this greenwashing?"** → No — GLEC/ISO 14083 method, book-and-claim wording, a specific *certified* figure, and we never say "carbon neutral." That precision *is* the campaign.
3. **"Why would an SME bother with the badge?"** → It helps them *sell* — ESG-claim products grow 28% vs 20%; it's 1-tap and margin-positive. A credential, not a chore.
4. **"How is the kg number credible?"** → MyDHL+ / GLEC-based; the QR shows the calculation; DHL itself is a GLEC member.
5. **"Does awareness actually change behaviour?"** → The tier + repeat loop turn awareness into habit; we measure *both* the awareness lift *and* the GoGreen Plus opt-in lift.
6. **"₿5M across 36 branches in 3 months — is that enough?"** → It buys *awareness*, not carbon; it reuses DHL's existing channels and activates a €7B asset. High leverage, not high spend.
7. **"Why not just a normal ad campaign?"** → A point-of-sale *felt moment* + the SME multiplier + Green-Claims mastery beat a generic awareness ad — and cost less.
8. **"Isn't 'flew on SAF' misleading?"** → Exactly why we say **"funded a verified reduction"** — book-and-claim. We got this right on purpose.
