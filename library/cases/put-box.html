Why this ties to green — the volumetric-weight rule

International express is charged on the greater of actual weight OR volumetric weight — i.e. DHL charges for the space you fly, not just the mass. So shipping a half-empty box = flying air you paid carbon for. Packing smart literally cuts cost and emissions.

volumetric weight (kg) = L × W × H (cm) ÷ 5000

How to run it WITHOUT wasting money

  • It's a game, not a flat-rate shipping offer — customers pack demo items into reusable boxes against the clock (nothing is actually shipped at a loss).
  • Reward = a small perk (free GoGreen recyclable packaging · tier points · a voucher · prize draw) — never free international shipping.
  • Cost ≈ ฿4k floor decal + ฿2k props + ~฿5k prizes per branch (one-time, reusable) → a roving activation at ~10 flagship branches ≈ ฿110k, well inside the ฿1.5M in-branch budget.
⚠️ Don't make it a real "buffet" flat-rate. International express is priced by weight + volumetric weight — a "stuff the box for ฿X" offer loses DHL money on heavy parcels and could blow the budget. Keep it a game with a perk, and score smart packing (least wasted space), not just speed — speed alone encourages over-packing, which is the opposite of the green message.
