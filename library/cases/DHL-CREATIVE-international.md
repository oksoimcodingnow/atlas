# 🎨 DHL Challenge — Creative & International Strategy

> The marketing-creative layer: how to make this **feel international**, **emotionally Thai**, and
> **award-worthy** — by borrowing proven moves from world-class campaigns and localizing DHL's own assets.
> Companions: [strategy bank](DHL-STRATEGY-bank.md) · [top 3](DHL-TOP3-deepdive.md) · [research](DHL-RESEARCH-deepdive.md).
> **Researched:** 23 Jun 2026. Sources at bottom.

## 0. THE CREATIVE UNLOCK — "international" isn't a constraint, it's the gift

This is **cross-border express shipping**. Every parcel literally *crosses borders.* So the green story can too.
Most teams will pitch a local awareness campaign. The winning creative move: **let the impact travel with
the parcel.**

> **Core creative idea:** *"Your kindness to the planet crosses borders with your parcel."*
> The sender (Thailand) ships green → a mark on the box → the **receiver abroad** sees it → DHL's green story
> reaches another country **for free.** One ฿36 shipment buys cross-border earned media.

That single reframe makes the campaign **international by design**, not by budget.

## 1. STEAL THESE — award-winning moves to borrow (with what to take)

| Campaign (award) | What they did | What to steal for DHL |
|---|---|---|
| **Renault "Cars to Work"** (Cannes 2024 SDG Grand Prix) | Didn't *advertise* mobility — *solved* a real barrier (free cars to reach job interviews) | Don't advertise "DHL is green" — **remove the barrier**: show customers they're *already* helping, free. Action > slogan. |
| **Natura "Amazon Greenventory"** (Cannes 2025 SDG) | Made the invisible visible — inventoried Amazon biodiversity into a usable asset | **Inventory the invisible CO₂** each customer cuts → the **Impact Counter**. Turn unseen reductions into a visible, shareable number. |
| **Thai Life / Thai insurance films** (perennial Cannes winners) | Emotional short-film storytelling — universal human moments (love for family, sacrifice) | Wrap the carbon number in a **Thai emotional story** (below). Numbers inform; emotion makes it *remembered*. |
| **HubSpot / IBM Partner Plus / Hyatt** (B2B2C badge programs) | Partners earn shareable badges/tiers + a live dashboard → become advocates | Validates the **Seller Badge** — SMEs earn "Ships greener with DHL," share it, become your channel. Proven model. |

## 2. THE THAI EMOTIONAL LAYER (your unfair advantage)

Thai advertising is **world-famous for emotional storytelling** — poignant, family-centred, universal.
Judges in Thailand reward this. Most case teams pitch dry logic; you can pitch **logic + a tear.**

**The emotional truth to build on:** people ship abroad to **stay connected to people they love** — a parcel
to a child studying overseas, a gift to family who emigrated, a product for a customer across the world.

> **The line:** *"You're not just sending a parcel across the world. You're protecting the world they live in too."*
> *(TH: "ไม่ใช่แค่ส่งของข้ามโลก แต่คุณกำลังดูแลโลกที่คนที่คุณรักอาศัยอยู่")*

Cross-border love + a shared planet = a story that's specific to DHL's product and impossible for a generic
"go green" ad to own.

## 3. MAKE-THE-INVISIBLE-VISIBLE — the creative toolkit (research-backed)

How to display the carbon number so it actually *lands* (all tested in research):
- **Relatable units** beat abstract kg — "≈ X km not driven," "≈ X phone charges," "= X trees for a day."
- **Traffic-light colour coding** — people react more strongly to green/amber/red than to numbers alone.
- **Monetary framing** — "you funded ฿X of real SAF" makes it concrete.
- **Gamified dashboards & feedback loops** *narrow the intention–behaviour gap sharply* — the Impact Counter is evidence-based, not decoration.
- **QR on packaging is on-trend & trusted** — sustainability/origin QR codes jumped **+88% YoY**; consumers actively want the "how." Your Green-Receipt QR rides a real behaviour wave.

## 4. THREE CREATIVE, INTERNATIONAL MECHANICS (built to travel)

### ✈️ A. "The Green Stamp that crosses borders"
A small mark/sticker on every GoGreen Plus box: *"This parcel helped cut CO₂ — shipped greener from Thailand."*
The **receiver abroad** sees it. Optional QR → a page (in the receiver's language) showing the cut + DHL's story.
→ **Sender feels pride, receiver discovers DHL green, in another country, free.** Cross-border word of mouth.

### 🌍 B. "Thailand's Green Shipping Map" (collective + international)
Localize **DHL's existing GoGreen Dashboard**: a live map showing CO₂ cut by ServicePoint shippers *and where
their parcels went* — a glowing web of green lines from Thailand to the world. Makes the collective impact
**visible, national, and visibly global.** (You're extending a real DHL tool — instant credibility.)

### 🤝 C. "Ships Greener with DHL" seller badge (B2B2C, borderless)
SMEs selling abroad earn a shareable badge for their shop/socials/packaging — proven by HubSpot/IBM/Hyatt
models. Their overseas buyers see it → DHL's green reputation spreads through *sellers' international audiences.*

> These three = **Feel → Share → Belong**, now with an international skin: the proof travels, the map goes
> global, the sellers reach abroad.

---

## 5. DHL ASSETS TO LOCALIZE (don't invent what exists)

- **GoGreen Dashboard** — DHL already runs a public emissions dashboard globally. **Localize it** for
  Thailand/ServicePoint instead of building from scratch. Huge feasibility + credibility win.
- **GoGreen Plus tiers (2026):** Base / Premium / Select, with **Base = 10% reduction via book-and-claim at a
  flat rate.** A flat, simple entry point is *perfect* for walk-in ServicePoint customers — lean on it.
- **GoGreen brand since 2008** — DHL's green equity is 18 years deep. You're amplifying a real, owned brand,
  not bolting on a stunt.

---

## 6. CAMPAIGN-NAME / SLOGAN DIRECTIONS (international flavour, TH + EN)

- **"Ship Love. Ship Light."** — *"ส่งความรัก ส่งอย่างรักษ์โลก"* (love + light footprint; cross-border warmth)
- **"Every Parcel, A Lighter Planet."** — *"ทุกพัสดุ ช่วยโลกเบาลง"*
- **"Your Green Crosses Borders."** — *"ความกรีนของคุณ ข้ามพรมแดนไปกับพัสดุ"*
- **"Sent with Care — for them, and for the planet."** — ties the emotional + the green in one line.

> Pick one that pairs a **human feeling** with a **specific, provable green action** — that combination is the
> winner-separator (and stays Green-Claims-safe).

---

## 7. HOW TO PITCH THE CREATIVE (so it scores)
1. Open with the **emotional cross-border truth** (a 20-second story) — earn attention the Thai way.
2. Reveal the **gap** (83% love DHL, nobody knows it's green) — the insight.
3. Land the **idea**: Green Receipt → Green Stamp (crosses borders) → Green Map → Seller Badge.
4. Prove **feasibility** by localizing DHL's *real* assets (Dashboard, GoGreen Plus Base tier).
5. Show **Green-Claims mastery** (book-and-claim, specific kg) — the credibility close.
6. End back on **emotion + a number**: "X tonnes cut, and a country that knows it shipped them."
