## ✅ LOCKED DECISIONS

### Big Idea: "Your Green Receipt" (ใบเสร็จสีเขียว)
Turn the receipt every shipper already gets into the **proof** that "you just helped the planet."
The green already exists — what's missing is the *moment the customer feels it.* The receipt
(physical + digital via LINE/email) carries a specific line: *"You funded a verified cut of X kg
CO₂ via sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) in DHL's network,"* with a QR explaining how (keeps it
Green-Claims-compliant). ⚠️ NOT "your parcel flew on SAF" — GoGreen Plus is **book-and-claim**, so
the specific parcel may not fly on SAF; the customer funds a *certified* reduction. See
[research deep-dive](DHL-RESEARCH-deepdive.md) §0.

### Target persona
- **PRIMARY: SME / online sellers** — why:
  1. Frequent shippers → the Green Receipt repeats → awareness actually builds (measurable)
  2. **Multiplier (B2B2C):** the seller can pass the green message to their OWN customers
     ("I ship greener with DHL" = their selling point). One receipt reaches seller + their buyers.
  3. Reachable in budget/time (LINE OA, seller groups, social commerce)
- **SECONDARY: Students** — for social shareability (#MyGreenReceipt) + next-gen shippers

### Evolution hook
Green Receipt → a **shareable badge** for sellers: *"This seller ships greener with DHL."*
Gives sellers *value*, not just info. Strong ROI angle.

---

## Why it wins the 5 judging criteria
- **Creativity:** reframes a boring receipt into a thank-you moment
- **Insight:** answers the Key Question at the exact point of action
- **Feasibility:** existing receipts + screens + LINE; no new tech, no celebs; fits ฿5M/36 branches/3mo
- **Impact/ROI:** QR scans, LINE opens, before/after awareness survey
- **Storytelling:** "the proof is in your hands"

## Green Claims safety
✅ specific kg CO₂ · ✅ QR explains SAF/insetting method · ❌ never "carbon neutral"/"emission-free"
