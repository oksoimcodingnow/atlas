# 📦 DHL × UOB Wonder Lab — "Ship Love. Ship Light."
### The case in short (read this — no links needed)

**THE PROBLEM**
DHL ServicePoint customers are very satisfied (**83%**) — but only with the *service*. They have **no idea
DHL does real green work**; it's invisible at the moment of shipping. *Key question (booklet): how do we make
customers realise that every time they ship at one of the 36 ServicePoints, they're helping the planet
alongside DHL?*

**OUR BIG IDEA — "Proof That Travels"**
We don't *tell* people DHL is green — we hand them **proof they personally helped**, and let it travel.
Campaign line: **"Ship Love. Ship Light."** (ส่งความรัก ส่งอย่างรักษ์โลก)

**THE SYSTEM — FEEL → CARRY → BELONG → SPREAD**
1. **FEEL · Green Receipt** — the receipt shows *"you funded a verified cut of ~340 g CO₂ ≈ 3 km not driven"* + a QR to see how it's calculated.
2. **CARRY · Green Stamp** — a mark on the box the receiver *abroad* sees → free cross-border reach.
3. **BELONG · Thailand's Green Map** — a live national total of CO₂ cut across all 36 branches.
4. **SPREAD · Seller Badge** — SMEs show *"Ships Greener with DHL"* to their own buyers (B2B2C multiplier).
   *(+ a Green Shipper tier — Sprout → Bronze → Silver → Gold by repeat green shipments — to make it a habit.)*

**WHO WE TARGET**
**Primary: Micro-SMEs / online sellers** (ship often + have their own audience = multiplier; "fairness for the
little guy" — mirrors UOB's SME mission). **Secondary: students** (shareable).

**WHY IT WORKS COMMERCIALLY (the money story)**
The ฿5M doesn't pay for carbon — **customers do** (via GoGreen Plus). The ฿5M buys **awareness** → more
opt-ins (high-margin for DHL) + sellers who sell better. **Green = a margin story, not a cost:**
- Products with green claims grew **28% vs 20%** (no claims) — McKinsey/NielsenIQ.
- Shoppers say they'll pay **~9.7% more** for sustainable goods — PwC.
*(UOB is a bank — they reward margin logic.)*

**WHY IT'S TRUSTWORTHY (the winner-separator)**
The number is **real and certified, not a marketing claim:**
- SAF (sustainable aviation fuel) = cleaner jet fuel from waste oil; cuts up to **~80% lifecycle CO₂**.
- It's **book-and-claim**: your exact parcel may not fly on SAF, so we say *"you funded a verified reduction,"*
  **never** *"your parcel flew on SAF."*
- Backed by **GLEC/ISO 14083** (the calc) · **ISCC/CORSIA** (the SAF cert) · **book-and-claim** (the allocation). DHL is a GLEC member.
- Green-Claims rule: specific + provable; **never** say "carbon neutral / emission-free / eco-friendly."

**THE STRATEGIC EDGE**
Most companies *"green-hush"* (stay quiet about green for fear of being called greenwashers). **DHL does the
opposite — breaks the silence with receipts.** Transparency builds trust (brands that admit limits get **+34%
trust**); 3rd-party certification is the **#1 trust source**.

**THE PITCH (6 beats):** emotion (a parcel sent to someone loved) → the gap (83% love DHL, nobody knows it's
green) → the idea → the system → the proof (GLEC/book-and-claim + DHL's real assets) → close (the UOB mirror +
*"X tonnes cut, and a country that knows"*).

**CONSTRAINTS:** ฿5M · 36 ServicePoints · 3 months · no celebrities.

**WHAT'S LEFT:** real DHL/MyDHL+ numbers · build the Canva deck · rehearse the pitch + Q&A.
