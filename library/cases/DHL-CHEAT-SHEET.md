# ⚡ DHL CHEAT SHEET — glance before the meeting

**Challenge:** *"Fast to the World. Kind to the Planet."* (DHL Express, UOB Wonder Lab)

## The problem in 1 line
Customers love DHL's service (**83% satisfied**) but **don't know DHL is green**. → Make them feel that *shipping abroad at a ServicePoint = helping the planet.*

## Numbers to drop
- **36** ServicePoint branches · **฿5M** budget · **3 months** · **NO celebrities**
- 83% satisfied (but only on service, not sustainability)
- TH: 43 EVs · solar on 13 sites · SAF 10% of plane fuel · Net Zero 2050

## The hero to promote
**GoGreen Plus** = customers cut carbon via **SAF** (sustainable aviation fuel). It already exists. Nobody knows. That's the gap.

## Personas (pick 1–2)
SMEs/online sellers · Expats · Students · Tourists

## ⚠️ Green Claims — DON'T get disqualified
- ❌ Never: "green / eco-friendly / sustainable" alone, "emission-free," "climate neutral"
- ✅ Always: specific %, explain *how*, plain language, provable
- **If you can't prove it, don't say it.**

## Judged on (20% each)
Creativity · Insight · Feasibility · Impact/ROI · Storytelling

## Your one-liner for the group
> "Everyone's satisfied but nobody knows DHL's green. Our job: turn the ServicePoint counter into a moment where the customer *feels* they just helped the planet — measurably, and within Green Claims rules."
