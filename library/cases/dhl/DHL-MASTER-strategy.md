## 1. THE UNIFIED THESIS (one paragraph)

DHL already does real, expensive, first-mover green work — but at the ServicePoint counter it's **invisible**,
so customers (83% satisfied) have no idea they're part of it. The fix isn't to *tell* them DHL is green; it's
to **hand them proof that they personally helped — and let that proof travel.** Because this is *cross-border*
shipping, the proof can cross borders too: it rides the parcel to a receiver abroad, adds to a visible national
total, and becomes a credential SME sellers broadcast to their own customers. One small, honest, specific act
of good — made **felt, visible, shareable, and collective** — at the exact moment of shipping.

## 2. THE BIG IDEA

# ✉️ "Proof That Travels"
### Campaign line (EN): **"Ship Love. Ship Light."**
### Campaign line (TH): **"ส่งความรัก ส่งอย่างรักษ์โลก"**

> *Double meaning:* every parcel already carries **love** across borders — now it carries a **lighter footprint**

**Why this idea and not just "awareness":** it converts a transaction into an **emotional, visible, portable

participation, Maersk dashboards), wrapped in Thailand's storytelling superpower.

## 3. THE SYSTEM — 4 connected pieces (Feel → Carry → Belong → Spread)

Each piece is one mechanic, now **woven with** a principle, the international angle, and the right claim.

### ① FEEL — "Your Green Receipt" *(the felt moment)*
- Every GoGreen Plus shipment prints a receipt (paper + LINE/email): **"You funded a verified cut of X kg CO₂
  via sustainable aviation fuel — ≈ Y km not driven."** (book-and-claim-correct; tangible unit; traffic-light colour).
- A **QR** explains *how* (SAF / insetting) — the Green-Claims shield + on-trend (sustainability QR +88% YoY).
- *Principle:* transparency = trust (Patagonia) + normalize the number (Allbirds/Oatly).

### ② CARRY — "The Green Stamp that crosses borders" *(international reach, free)*
- A small mark on the box: *"Shipped greener from Thailand — this parcel helped cut CO₂."* The **receiver abroad**
  sees it; optional QR in their language tells DHL's story.
- *Principle:* customers as broadcasters + the parcel literally crosses borders → **cross-border earned media for ฿0.**

### ③ BELONG — "Thailand's Green Shipping Map" *(collective pride)*
- Localize **DHL's real GoGreen Dashboard** into a live map: CO₂ cut by ServicePoint shippers + glowing lines
  from Thailand to the world. On branch screens + online; national total across 36 branches.
- *Principle:* make the invisible visible & collective (IKEA/EcoCart) + proven, low-risk (Maersk/Lufthansa dashboards).

### ④ SPREAD — "Ships Greener with DHL" Seller Badge *(the multiplier + the UOB mirror)*
- SME sellers who ship green unlock a **shareable badge** (LINE OA) for their shop/socials/packaging → their
  overseas buyers see it → DHL's green spreads through sellers' *international* audiences.
- *Principle:* B2B2C broadcaster model (HubSpot/IBM/Hyatt) + **mirrors UOB's "Sustainability Compass for SMEs."**

> **They plug together:** the Receipt's data feeds the Map; the Stamp carries it abroad; the Badge makes sellers
> spread it. One system, four reinforcing touchpoints. **Feel → Carry → Belong → Spread.**

## 4. THE CUSTOMER JOURNEY (end to end, cross-border)

```
Walk into ServicePoint
   → staff one-liner: "want this to ship greener? you'll get proof you helped."  (free, human)
   → choose GoGreen Plus (Base tier = flat-rate 10%, simple for walk-ins)
   → GREEN RECEIPT prints: "you cut X kg CO₂ ≈ Y km not driven" + QR        ① FEEL
   → box leaves with the GREEN STAMP                                         ② CARRY
   → receiver abroad sees the stamp → scans → discovers DHL green   (free international reach)
   → customer sees the national MAP tick up on the branch screen / online    ③ BELONG
   → (SME) unlocks the SELLER BADGE in LINE → posts it to their shop         ④ SPREAD
   → their buyers prefer the green seller → seller ships green again → repeat → Map grows
```

## 6. THE ฿5M / 3-MONTH ROLLOUT (phased)

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Rough budget bucket |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Build & brief** | 1–3 | Receipt + stamp design, QR/landing pages, LINE integration, localize the Map, staff one-liner script, **baseline awareness survey** | setup + creative |
| **2 · Launch at the counter** | 4–8 | Go live across **36 branches**; in-branch screens/posters/counter cards; LINE push; seed the Map | rollout + in-branch media |
| **3 · Spread & prove** | 9–12 | Seller Badge to SME communities; UGC (#ShipLight); milestone moments on the Map; **post survey + ROI readout** | amplification + measurement |

*Guardrail:* ฿5M ÷ 36 branches ÷ 3 months ≈ **฿46k/branch/month** — favour cheap, repeating touchpoints
(receipt, screen, staff line, LINE) over one-off spectacle. **No presenter/celebrity (not in budget, p14).**
