## 0. THE 6 TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLES (the deep insight)

Across every great campaign, awareness is built one of six ways. Our idea spine already hits all six —
which is *why* it's strong. Name these in the pitch:

1. **Radical specificity / transparency builds trust.** Show the real number, even the ugly one.
2. **Make the customer a participant, not an audience.** "You did this," not "we did this."
3. **Make the invisible visible & tangible.** A number on the product; impact as trees/km.
4. **Turn customers into broadcasters.** Their pride = your free reach.
5. **Give a simple proof/dashboard.** A place to *see* the collective impact.
6. **Be provocative / set a standard.** A bold hook earns the attention awareness needs.

## 1. THE BENCHMARK TABLE (company → move → lesson for DHL)

| Company | What they did | The lesson → DHL application |
|---|---|---|
| **Patagonia** — "Don't Buy This Jacket" | Black-Friday ad telling people *not* to buy; listed the jacket's real harm (135 L water, 20 lb CO₂). Sales rose ~30%. | **Transparency = trust.** Be specific & honest (book-and-claim, exact kg). Honesty is the Green-Claims shield *and* a trust magnet. |
| **Patagonia** — Worn Wear | 415,000+ items repaired; customers proudly post their patched gear (UGC). | **Customers as broadcasters.** → Seller Badge + #MyGreenShipment. Pride travels. |
| **Oatly** — "Show us your numbers" | Put a climate footprint on every pack; dared the whole industry to do the same. | **Provocation + a number.** A bold, specific hook earns attention. → the Green Receipt's number as a talking point. |
| **Allbirds** — carbon labels | Carbon footprint on every shoe, "like a nutrition label." | **Normalize the number.** Make the CO₂ figure routine & glanceable on the receipt/screen. |
| **TOMS / Bombas** — one-for-one | "Every purchase does good" — bought emotional participation. | **Participation > information.** "Every shipment helped" reframes a transaction as a good deed. |

| **EcoCart / Ecosia** (e-commerce checkout) | Offset-at-checkout with **live impact counters** + tree equivalence; **+10% cart conversion**; H&M/Goop offset 4M lb CO₂. | **Impact counters & tangible units WORK** — and even lift conversion. ## 2. DEEP CUTS — the four most relevant, unpacked

### ⭐ Patagonia (the trust masterclass)
The counter-intuitive genius: by being **brutally honest about its own footprint**, Patagonia became the most
*trusted* brand in its category — and grew. For DHL this is the answer to the Green-Claims trap: **specificity
and honesty aren't a legal burden, they're the marketing.** "You funded a verified cut of X kg (here's exactly
how)" out-trusts any competitor's vague "we're eco-friendly."

### ⭐ Carbon labels — Oatly / Allbirds / Quorn (make the number normal)
The movement is to make a CO₂ figure **as routine as a nutrition label**. The receipt/screen number isn't a
gimmick — it's where consumer expectation is already heading. Oatly went further and made *transparency itself*
the campaign ("show us your numbers"). DHL could own "the first courier to show you your shipment's number."

### ⭐ EcoCart / Ecosia (proof the Impact Counter converts)
This is the strongest evidence for our mechanics: live **impact counters**, **tree/km equivalence**, and
**individual + collective tracking** are already deployed at checkout — and they **raised conversion ~10%**.
So the Impact Counter isn't just awareness; it can *grow GoGreen Plus uptake* (your ROI story).

### ⭐ Maersk + Lufthansa (your direct industry proof)
DHL's rivals-in-spirit already do this: Maersk gives customers an **emissions dashboard**; Lufthansa lets
flyers **fund SAF voluntarily**. → Localizing DHL's GoGreen Dashboard for Thai ServicePoints is **proven,
low-risk, industry-standard** — strong feasibility argument.

## 3. ⭐ THE UOB ANGLE — reflect the sponsor's own mission (tailoring win)

The judge is **UOB**. Show you know what *they* care about:
- UOB is committed to **net-zero by 2050** and frames it as a **"just transition" for Southeast Asia.**
- UOB runs a **"Sustainability Compass"** program **to help SMEs start their sustainability journey** (built with PwC).
- UOB Thailand approved **฿33.96B in sustainable finance (2024)** and won **Best SME Bank for Sustainable Finance – Thailand**.

**Why this is gold:** your **primary persona is SMEs.** So your campaign — helping SME shippers *participate in
and broadcast* sustainability — **directly echoes UOB's own SME-sustainability mission.** A line like:
> *"Just as UOB's Sustainability Compass helps SMEs start their green journey, our campaign lets SME shippers
> *prove* theirs — every parcel becomes a credential they can show their own customers."*

…tells the judges you understood not just DHL, but **why UOB is hosting this.** Few teams will do that.

## 4. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR OUR PITCH
- We're not guessing — **every mechanic has a proven precedent** (cite them: "as Patagonia/Oatly/EcoCart/Maersk did…").
- Lead with **transparency-as-trust** (Patagonia/Oatly) → it doubles as Green-Claims compliance.
- Use **impact counters + tangible units** (EcoCart) → proven to convert, not just inform.
- Frame customers as **participants & broadcasters** (TOMS/IKEA/Worn Wear) → the SME multiplier.
- **Localize DHL's real dashboard** (Maersk/Lufthansa precedent) → feasibility.
- **Mirror UOB's SME-sustainability mission** → win the sponsor's heart.
