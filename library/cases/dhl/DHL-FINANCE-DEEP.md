## HOW TO USE THIS IN THE ROOM
- Slide shows the **6-bucket donut** (clean). This doc is the **back-up** for "break that down."
- If a judge picks any line: *"That's [unit] × [qty] = [subtotal], from [vendor]. Items marked est. are
  benchmarked, not quoted — we'd firm them at RFP in Phase 1."*
- ⚠️ **Honesty rule:** the `[est.]` flags are deliberate. Claiming invoice-precision on a 3-month plan you
  haven't tendered would be *less* credible, not more. "Sourced where public, benchmarked where not, firmed
  at RFP" is the answer a finance panel respects.
