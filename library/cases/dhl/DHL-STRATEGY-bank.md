# 🧠 DHL Challenge — Strategy Bank (deep)

> Broad idea bank + deep build-outs. Pick / mix / optimize later with the team.
> Anchored to the case: [summary](DHL-Express-UOB-WonderLab-2026.md) · [cheat sheet](DHL-CHEAT-SHEET.md)
> **The job:** raise *awareness* that shipping abroad at a DHL ServicePoint = helping the planet.
> **Constraints:** ฿5M · 36 branches · 3 months · no celebrities · Green-Claims-compliant.

---

## PART A — THE INSIGHT LAYER (the "why" any idea must hit)

Every idea below is judged against the **real problem**: not "DHL isn't green," but
**"the green is invisible at the moment of action."** So the winning lever is almost always:
**create a felt MOMENT at the counter + a reason to remember/share it.**

Three strategic angles you can build a campaign around. Most strong ideas are one of these:

1. **Make the invisible visible** — surface the green impact the customer is already causing.
2. **Give the customer a role** — they're not a bystander, they're a co-contributor ("you + DHL").
3. **Give them something to carry/share** — proof that travels (receipt, badge, sticker, post).

---

## PART B — THE BIG IDEA BANK (breadth — 12 directions)

> Don't evaluate yet, just see the field. Deep build-outs of the best are in Part C.

### Make-the-invisible-visible
1. **Your Green Receipt** — receipt shows "you funded a verified cut of X kg CO₂ via SAF" + QR. *(built out)*
2. **The Impact Counter** — a live screen at each branch + online: "customers here cut ___ kg CO₂ this month."
3. **Carbon-to-something tangible** — translate kg CO₂ into relatable units ("= 3 trees for a day," "= X km not driven"). Easier to feel than abstract kg.

### Give-the-customer-a-role
4. **"You + DHL" co-badge** — a moment/asset framing the customer as a partner in the reduction, not a buyer.
5. **Green Streak / loyalty** — track repeat green shipments; small rewards for milestones (works great for SME repeat shippers).
6. **Pledge wall** — at the counter or digital, customers "join" the green-shipping movement; visible count of participants.

### Give-them-something-to-carry/share
7. **The Green Stamp/Sticker on the box** — sender feels proud; receiver abroad sees it → cross-border word of mouth.
8. **Shareable seller badge** — SME sellers get "Ships greener with DHL" to put on their own shop/socials. *(built out)*
9. **#MyGreenShipment UGC** — customers post their green receipt/box; light social campaign, student-friendly.

### Channel / format plays
10. **ServicePoint takeover** — redesign the in-branch experience (screens, posters, counter cards, staff script) into one coherent green story across 36 branches.
11. **LINE OA green journey** — turn the existing LINE OA into the digital home: impact dashboard, tips, the digital receipt.
12. **Edutainment content series** — short, myth-busting clips ("is green shipping slower/pricier? no") that kill the hidden objection.

## PART C — DEEP BUILD-OUTS (the strongest few, fully developed)

### ⭐ IDEA 1 — "Your Green Receipt" (ใบเสร็จสีเขียว)
**Angle:** make-the-invisible-visible + give-a-role.
**Mechanic:** every ServicePoint shipment (esp. GoGreen Plus/SAF) produces a receipt — physical AND
digital (LINE/email) — carrying a specific line: *"You funded a verified cut of X kg CO₂ via sustainable
aviation fuel (SAF) in DHL's network"* plus a QR that explains the calculation (SAF / insetting /
book-and-claim). Optional running personal total. ⚠️ Wording matters — book-and-claim means the parcel
may not physically fly on SAF; claim the *certified reduction*, not "flew on SAF."
**Customer journey:** walk in → ship → staff says "this one helped cut CO₂" → receive Green Receipt →
scan QR (see how) → keep/screenshot → (SME) reuse as proof to own customers → return next time.
**Why it scores:**
- Creativity: a boring receipt becomes a thank-you moment.
- Insight: hits the Key Question at the exact point of action.
- Feasibility: uses existing receipts + screens + LINE; no new tech, no celebs; fits ฿5M/36/3mo.
- Impact/ROI: QR scans, LINE opens, repeat rate, before/after awareness survey.
- Storytelling: "the proof is in your hands."
**Green Claims safety:** specific kg number, QR explains method (incl. book-and-claim), never "carbon neutral" or "flew on SAF."
**Budget sketch (฿5M / 3mo):** receipt+screen redesign & rollout to 36 branches · QR landing page +
LINE integration · staff briefing · awareness survey (pre/post) · social amplification of real receipts.
**Risks & answers:**
- *"A receipt gets thrown away."* → that's why it's also digital (LINE/email) + the in-store screen + staff line; the receipt is one touchpoint of several.
- *"How is the kg number credible?"* → QR shows the methodology; compliant with Green Claims (specific + provable).
- *"Does it change behavior or just inform?"* → the running total + seller badge create repeat + advocacy, not just a one-time read.

### ⭐ IDEA 2 — "Ships Greener with DHL" seller badge (B2B2C multiplier)
**Angle:** give-them-something-to-carry/share, aimed at SME sellers (primary persona).
**Mechanic:** SME sellers who ship via GoGreen Plus get a verified digital **badge** + assets
(IG sticker, shop banner, packaging insert) saying "Ships greener with DHL." They display it to
*their* customers, turning DHL's sustainability into the seller's selling point.
**Why it's powerful:** one DHL message reaches the seller AND the seller's whole audience (multiplier).
Sellers WANT it because it helps them sell. Cheap to produce, scales by itself.
**Journey:** seller ships green → unlocks badge in LINE OA → adds it to shop/socials → their buyers see
it → buyers prefer the green seller → seller ships more with DHL.
**ROI:** badge claims, reach via seller audiences (earned, not paid), repeat shipping volume.
**Green Claims safety:** badge must link to what it means (GoGreen Plus + method); avoid implying the
seller's whole business is "carbon neutral."
**Risk/answer:** *"Will sellers bother?"* → tie it to a tangible sell-through benefit + make it 1-tap in LINE.

### IDEA 3 — "The Impact Counter" (collective visibility)
**Angle:** make-the-invisible-visible, community/gamified.
**Mechanic:** a live tally at each branch + online: "Customers at this ServicePoint helped cut ___ kg
CO₂ this month / this year." Turns individual acts into a shared, visible win; creates a return reason
("did my shipment count?").
**Pairs well with:** Idea 1 (the receipt feeds the counter).
**ROI:** dwell/engagement at screen, online dashboard visits, social screenshots.
**Risk:** needs real data plumbing → keep numbers honest & sourced (Green Claims).

### IDEA 4 — "Same speed. Lighter footprint." (objection-killer campaign)
**Angle:** kill the hidden belief that green = slower or pricier.
**Mechanic:** content + in-store messaging proving you get DHL's famous speed AND lower footprint
(SAF doesn't slow delivery). Short myth-bust clips, counter cards, LINE posts.
**Why:** addresses the unspoken reason customers don't engage with green options.
**ROI:** message recall, intent-to-use GoGreen Plus, survey shift.

## PART D — SUPPORTING LAYERS (mix into any Big Idea)

**Mechanics / "felt moment" devices**
- Carbon → relatable units (trees, km, phone charges) so the number means something
- A small physical token (sticker/stamp) the customer leaves with
- A "you've cut X total" personal counter for repeat shippers
- Staff one-liner script (free, human, high-impact at the counter)

**Channels (all within existing assets — feasibility win)**
- In-branch: ServicePoint screens, posters, counter cards, staff script (36 branches)
- Digital: LINE OA (DHL Express Shop), Facebook/IG (DHL Express Thailand)
- Earned: customer/seller sharing (UGC), seller audiences (B2B2C)
- Receipt/packaging as media (nearly free, every shipment)

**Partnerships (low/no-cost, no celebrities)**
- Online seller communities / marketplaces (reach SMEs)
- Universities / student orgs (secondary persona, social reach)
- Expat groups / chambers of commerce (expat persona)

**KPIs / measurement (judges weight this 20%)**
- Awareness lift: pre vs. post survey at ServicePoints (the case itself uses survey data — mirror it)
- Engagement: QR scans, LINE opens/dashboard visits, badge claims
- Behavior: GoGreen Plus uptake, repeat green-shipping rate
- Earned reach: UGC posts, seller-audience impressions
- Always tie back to ฿ spent → ROI/cost-effectiveness (the case explicitly asks for this)

**Risk register (have answers ready)**
- Greenwashing accusation → strict Green Claims compliance, methodology shown
- "Awareness ≠ action" → build repeat + advocacy loops, not one-time reads
- 3-month / ฿5M limits → reuse existing channels, prioritize the counter moment
- Data credibility → only specific, sourced, provable numbers

## PART E — HOW TO OPTIMIZE LATER (decision filter)
When the team narrows, score each idea on the **5 judging criteria** AND the **constraints**:
1. Does it create a *felt moment* (insight)?  2. Buildable in ฿5M/36/3mo with no celebs (feasibility)?
3. Measurable (impact/ROI)?  4. Distinctive (creativity)?  5. One clean story (storytelling)?
6. Bonus: does it show you understood Green Claims? (separates winners)

> Strongest spine so far: **Green Receipt (felt moment) + Seller Badge (multiplier) + Impact Counter
> (collective proof)** — they reinforce each other and cover all 5 criteria. Optimize from there.
