# 🎯 DHL Challenge — Back to the Brief (it's an AWARENESS campaign)

> Night-before refocus. The brief asks ONE thing: **a campaign to raise awareness that DHL is doing green
> things.** Not a new product, not a supply-chain redesign, not a finance thesis. This doc realigns
> everything to that, and says what to CUT. Researched 27 Jun 2026.

---

## 1. WHAT THE BRIEF ACTUALLY ASKS
> "Make a campaign that raises awareness DHL is doing green things."
- Deliverable = **awareness lift** (people know + feel DHL is green), within ฿5M / 36 branches / 3 months.
- NOT asked: invent a product (GoGreen Plus exists), fix logistics, build a bank. Those are *context*, not the ask.
- ✅ Our answer fits perfectly: **"Proof That Travels"** = make DHL's invisible green work *felt, visible,
  shareable.* That IS an awareness campaign.

## 2. WHAT THE RESEARCH SAYS WINS A GREEN-AWARENESS CAMPAIGN
Every source agrees on the same 5 levers (and we already do all 5):
1. **Proof, not promises** — consumers want *numbers + certificates*, not vibes. → our Green Receipt (verified kg).
2. **Make it tangible/visible** — turn invisible impact into something you can see. → receipt, map, badge.
3. **Specific action, not just awareness** — tell people exactly what to do. → "choose GoGreen Plus, get proof."
4. **Authentic story + real people** — narrative beats stats alone. → Thai SME story, ServicePoint video.
5. **Avoid greenwashing** — specific, substantiated claims only. → book-and-claim precision (our trump card).

➡️ **We're not missing anything. We're carrying extra weight.** The job now is to CUT, not add.

## 3. THE CAMPAIGN IN ONE SENTENCE (the north star)
> *"We make DHL's invisible green work visible, at the moment of shipping, and let the proof travel —
> so customers know, feel, and spread that they helped."*
Everything in the deck must serve that sentence. If it doesn't, it's a back-up slide or it's cut.

## 4. ✂️ THE CUT LIST (tighten the deck to the brief)
KEEP in the main flow (these ARE the awareness campaign):
- Emotion · the 83% gap · the insight · the Big Idea · the 4-piece system (Feel/Carry/Belong/Spread) ·
  the precedent (proof it works) · the ฿5M plan · the close. **That's the campaign.**

MOVE TO APPENDIX (real, but not the ask — only if a judge probes):
- Tier scoring **math** (keep the tier *idea* in main; the formula is appendix).
- SME categorization tables (OSMEP/EU/World Bank) → appendix.
- Full ecosystem value-chain → appendix (keep ONE line in main: "backed by a real, audited SAF chain").
- Deep finance line-items + sensitivity → appendix (keep the donut + one ROI line in main).
- Policy/PDPA/EmpCo detail → appendix (keep ONE line in main: "compliant by design").

CUT or compress (scope creep for an *awareness* brief):
- Long competitive benchmark vs FedEx/UPS → compress to the single "43 vs 12 EVs" stat on the Numbers slide.
- Carbon/SAF *science* depth → one line ("verified SAF reductions"); the chemistry isn't the campaign.
- Anything about redesigning DHL operations, building new tech, or multi-year roadmaps → not asked, cut.

## 5. THE TEST FOR EVERY SLIDE
Ask: **"Does this help people KNOW, FEEL, or SPREAD that DHL is green?"**
- Yes → main flow. No, but defends a question → appendix. No at all → cut.
