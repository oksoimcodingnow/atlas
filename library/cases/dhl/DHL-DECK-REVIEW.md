## 🔴 TOP PRIORITY FIXES (do these first — they're what a judge attacks)

1. **Reconcile the carbon number.** Slide 16 shows **500,000 kg CO₂** target at **฿10/kg**. Problem: we also
   say carbon cost to DHL = ฿0 (customers fund it via GoGreen Plus). So "฿10/kg" reads as ambiguous.
   → FIX: relabel it **"฿10 per kg CO₂ of *awareness spend*"** (= ฿5M ÷ 500,000 kg), and state plainly:
   "this is marketing cost-efficiency, not the cost of the carbon itself — customers fund the carbon."

2. **Check "2.1 million metric tons reduced in 2025" (slide 3).** This needs a source or it's a landmine.
   Our _PROOF has: DHL used **185 kt SAF** in 2025; group target **<29 Mt total** by 2030. "2.1M tonnes
   reduced" is not a figure we verified. → FIX: source it from DHL's report, or replace with a ✅ number
   from _PROOF (e.g. "185 kt SAF in 2025, ~10% of jet fuel, top-3 global buyer").

3. **One awareness target, not two.** Slide 12 roadmap says **15% → 45%**; slides 13/16 say **70%**.
   → FIX: pick one. Recommend "**awareness 15% → 45%**" as the lift (measurable, conservative) and reserve
   "70% of Micro-SMEs feel they participate" as a *separate* engagement goal — but label them differently so
   they don't look contradictory.

4. **Defend 500,000 kg CO₂ feasibility.** 500 tonnes in 3 months across 36 branches at ~10% opt-in is the
   biggest "prove it" risk. → Have the back-of-envelope ready: (opt-ins × avg kg per shipment). If it
   doesn't math out, lower the target to what's defensible. A smaller real number beats a big shaky one.
