## 0. THE ONE-LINE MONEY THESIS
> Our ฿5M doesn't pay for the carbon — **customers do** (via GoGreen Plus). The ฿5M buys *awareness*,
> awareness drives *opt-ins*, opt-ins are **high-margin revenue** for DHL and a **conversion lift** for
> sellers. Green here isn't a cost centre — it's a margin story for everyone in the chain.

---

## 1. THE MARGIN FOR **DHL** (why the campaign pays for itself)

### a) GoGreen Plus is sold at the cost of abatement — it's a clean, scalable revenue line
- DHL prices GoGreen Plus at **~€325 per tonne CO₂e** (2023), = the market value of the abatement; customers
  can dial reduction from **10%–100%** (one click = 30% via SAF). [DHL / ad-hoc-news]
- This means every opt-in is **incremental revenue on an existing shipment** — near-zero extra cost to sell
  (the parcel was shipping anyway). The campaign's job is simply to **raise the opt-in rate.**

### b) The awareness → opt-in → revenue chain (the ROI logic)
```
฿5M awareness spend
   → more ServicePoint customers KNOW GoGreen Plus exists & feel good using it
   → opt-in rate rises (today: low, because awareness is low — the case's whole problem)
   → each opt-in = GoGreen Plus revenue (≈€325/tonne) at high margin
   → + retention/loyalty halo on the core (premium, trusted) shipping product
```
**ROI framing for the pitch:** "We don't ask DHL to *spend* on carbon — customers fund that. We spend on
*awareness*, and awareness converts to a high-margin green-product revenue line DHL already built."

### c) The premium-positioning halo (harder to measure, real)
- 80% of consumers say they'll pay more for sustainable goods; stated **9.7% willingness-to-pay premium**
  (PwC 2024) — even amid cost-of-living worry. [PwC]
- For a brand already winning on *service* (83% satisfied), adding a *credible* green reason-to-choose
  protects pricing power vs. cheaper couriers. Sustainability becomes a **moat, not a cost.**

### d) Honest caveat (say it before a judge does)
- Stated WTP ≠ actual spend; inflation makes people trade down. **That's exactly why our frame is
  "you're already helping, for free, just by shipping"** — we don't rely on customers paying more; the
  GoGreen Plus opt-in is small and optional, and the *awareness/pride* is free. Margin upside is the
  bonus, not the dependency.

## 2. THE MARGIN FOR THE **SME SELLER** (why they'll actually spread the badge)

This is the heart of the multiplier: **sellers broadcast the badge because it makes THEM money.**

### a) Green claims correlate with faster sales growth (the headline stat)
- McKinsey × NielsenIQ analysed **$400B / 600,000 SKUs / 5 yrs**: products with ESG-related claims grew
  **28% cumulative vs 20%** for those without, and accounted for **56% of all growth** (~18% more than
  expected). In **two-thirds of categories**, claim-makers grew faster. Products with **multiple** claims
  grew **~2× faster**. [McKinsey/NielsenIQ 2023]
- **Translation for the seller:** "Ships greener with DHL" is a *claim* you can put on your shop — and
  claims like it track with measurably faster growth.

### b) Eco options at checkout lift conversion (direct e-commerce proof)
- Merchants adding sustainability at checkout report **+10–25% conversion**; case studies: **+22%** cart
  conversion (Nuzest), **+19%** (Tribe Kelley) — *even when only ~1 in 10 buyers actually opted in.*
  [EcoCart / The Good API]
- **The killer insight:** the lift comes from the *signal of values*, not from everyone choosing green.
  So a seller's "Ships greener with DHL" badge can lift their conversion **even if most of their buyers
  never think about carbon.** The badge is a trust/credibility asset.

### c) Marketplaces are formalizing it
- Amazon/Etsy already run **sustainability badges** and may factor them into **search ranking.** [Baymard/nshift]
- A DHL-verified green credential = a **free, ready-made badge** sellers can use across their channels →
  better trust, better ranking, better conversion. DHL gives them a sales tool; they spread DHL's message.

### d) The seller's P&L, plainly
```
Cost to seller:    ~0 (badge is free; GoGreen Plus surcharge is small & optional, often passed to buyer)
Benefit to seller: trust + conversion lift (+10–25% range) + claim that tracks with faster growth
                   + marketplace badge/ranking upside
→ Net: a margin-positive credential. That's WHY the multiplier works — it's self-interested, not charity.
```

## 3. THE TWO-SIDED FLYWHEEL (put this on a slide)
```
DHL spends ฿5M on AWARENESS
      ↓
Customers learn + feel good → GoGreen Plus opt-ins ↑  → DHL green revenue ↑ (high margin)
      ↓                                                        ↓
SME sellers get a verified BADGE  ───────────────→  sellers' conversion ↑ (+10–25%)
      ↓                                                        ↓
sellers broadcast it to their buyers (free reach) → more green shipping → DHL data/Map grows
      ↓
awareness compounds → opt-ins compound → everyone's margin compounds
```
**One sentence:** *awareness is the only thing we buy; margin is what it manufactures — on both sides.*

---

## 4. WHY A BANK (UOB) LOVES THIS
- It's framed as **unit economics + ROI**, not vibes: cost (awareness) → mechanism (opt-in/conversion) →
  return (high-margin green revenue + seller growth).
- It mirrors **UOB's own SME-sustainability mission** (helping SMEs go green) — but adds the part banks
  care about most: **it makes the SME more profitable**, so the green behaviour is *sustainable* (pun intended)
  because it pays for itself.
- It respects the **value–action gap**: we don't bet the case on people paying more; the margin is upside
  on top of a free-to-feel-good base.
