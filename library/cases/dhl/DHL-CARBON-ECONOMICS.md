## 🌍 1. THE CONTEXT — where shipping/aviation sits in global emissions

- **Aviation ≈ 1.3–2.4%** of global CO₂ (≈ **882 Mt in 2024**, and **growing ~8%/yr** — fast).
- International **aviation + shipping ≈ 3%** of global GHG, projected to balloon to **15–40% by 2040**
  if unchecked (other sectors decarbonize faster).
- Only **0.25% of freight tonnage goes by air** — but it's the **most carbon-intensive mode per kg**, which
  is exactly why **express air shipping** is a meaningful place to cut. *(This justifies the whole campaign:
  small volume, big per-parcel footprint → reducing it matters.)*

---

## 🪜 2. THE COST LADDER — what it costs to cut 1 tonne of CO₂ (the key comparison)

| Method | Cost per tonne CO₂ | Quality / notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cheap offsets (REDD+, forestry) | **~$3–14** | low-rated, "avoidance," weak credibility, **banned for "neutral" claims under EU EmpCo 2026** |
| Nature-based removals | **~$20** | better, still offsetting |
| Blended corporate offset portfolio | **~$25–80** | typical company average |
| **SAF / insetting (what DHL does)** | **~$300** | **in-sector, real, additional, certified — survives 2026 rules** |
| Direct air capture (DAC) | **$100–1,000+** | gold standard, not scalable yet |

### ⭐ THE INSIGHT THAT WINS POINTS
**SAF abatement (~$300/tonne) costs roughly 15–100× more than cheap offsets (~$3–20/tonne).**
DHL **deliberately pays that premium** because SAF is a *real, in-its-own-supply-chain* reduction
(**insetting**), not a cheap offset bought elsewhere. So when the campaign says "you helped cut CO₂," it's
backed by the **expensive, credible** kind of reduction — the kind that **survives the EmpCo 2026 ban on
offset-based claims.**

> Pitch line: *"DHL didn't take the cheap offset shortcut. It pays ~$300 a tonne for real SAF reductions.
> Our campaign's credibility is bought and paid for — that's why we can say it out loud."*

## ⛽ 3. WHAT SAF ACTUALLY COSTS (why the premium exists)

- SAF was **~3.1× jet-fuel price in 2024**, rising to **~4.2× in 2025**.
- **€2,085/tonne SAF** vs **€734/tonne** conventional jet fuel (2024).
- Production cost ranges **€1,461/tonne (biofuel) → €7,695/tonne (e-fuel)**.
- Still only **~0.7% of global jet fuel**; price stays **2–3× fossil until ~2030**.
- → This is why GoGreen Plus has a surcharge: the customer is funding a genuinely pricier fuel.

## 🧮 4. THE CUSTOMER-CONTRIBUTION MODEL (the part you asked for)

**DHL's GoGreen Plus price point:** roughly **€325 per tonne CO₂e** (≈ **฿0.30–0.40 per kg CO₂**, FX-dependent).
The surcharge is calculated from **shipment weight × route × the volume of SAF needed** for the chosen
reduction level. One click on MyDHL+ = **30% reduction**; customizable **10–100%**; contract up to **80%**.

### Worked example (ILLUSTRATIVE — replace with MyDHL+ actuals)
```
Assume a 2 kg parcel, Thailand → Europe, emits ≈ 10 kg CO₂  (← GET REAL kg FROM MyDHL+)
Customer picks GoGreen Plus one-click = 30% reduction
   → cut ≈ 3 kg CO₂ on that shipment
Cost at ~€325/tonne (= €0.325/kg ≈ ฿12/kg CO₂):
   3 kg × ฿12  ≈  ฿36 extra   (a few % of an international express price)
```
**So one customer contributes ≈ a few kg of CO₂ cut for ≈ tens of baht.** Tiny individually — which is
*exactly* the behavioral problem ("my one parcel?") that the **Impact Counter** (collective total) solves.

### Scale it for the campaign (the slide math)
```
Campaign goal: lift GoGreen Plus uptake at the 36 ServicePoints.
If the awareness campaign converts N extra customers/month to opt in,
   monthly CO₂ cut ≈ N × (avg kg cut per shipment)
   annual story    ≈ N × 12 × avg kg
Tie this to the ฿5M / 3-month budget → "cost per extra aware customer" and
   "cost per extra tonne of CO₂ enabled" = your ROI numbers.
```

## 📊 5. HOW THE CAMPAIGN ROI ACTUALLY WORKS (important framing)

The **฿5M does NOT buy carbon** — it buys **awareness**. The carbon is funded by *customers* choosing
GoGreen Plus. So your ROI is:

- **Cost side:** ฿5M / 3 months / 36 branches ≈ **฿46,000 per branch per month**.
- **Return side (measure these):**
  1. **GoGreen Plus uptake lift** — % of ServicePoint customers opting in, before vs after.
  2. **Awareness lift** — pre/post survey (mirror the booklet's own 83% survey method).
  3. **Cost per aware customer** = ฿5M ÷ (customers reached × awareness lift).
  4. **CO₂ enabled** = incremental opt-ins × avg kg cut → a tangible "campaign helped cut X tonnes" story.
  5. **Earned reach** (seller badge / UGC) — free amplification = ROI multiplier.

> The honest, judge-proof line: *"We don't claim ฿5M cuts carbon. We claim ฿5M makes thousands of
> customers realise they can — and measurably more of them do."*
