- TITLE: Feel → Carry → Belong → Spread
- Cards: ① FEEL Green Receipt "proof you helped, in your hand" · ② CARRY Green Stamp "the receiver abroad sees it" · ③ BELONG Green Map "a national total, ticking up" · ④ SPREAD Seller Badge "SMEs broadcast it to buyers"

- 🎤 Four connected pieces. Receipt makes you feel it; stamp carries it abroad; map makes it collective; badge spreads it. The Tier brings them back. One loop.

**7 · FEEL (deep)**
- TITLE: A receipt turns an invisible cut into proof you can hold.
- Body: "You funded a verified cut of X kg CO₂ ≈ ~6 km not driven" + QR explains how (book-and-claim correct)

**8 · CARRY + BELONG**

**9 · SPREAD**

- 🎤 The little guy becomes the broadcaster. That's the multiplier — and the UOB mirror.
