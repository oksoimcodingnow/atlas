### 2. SITUATION ANALYSIS (read first with the team)

**One line:** *DHL already does the green work and customers already love DHL — they just don't know the
two are connected. Connect them at the moment of shipping, compliantly, and let the customers who ship
most carry the message.*

**5C:** Company = market leader, real first-mover green creds, has done the work but never told it at the
counter · Customers = come for speed not green; SMEs highest value (repeat + own audience) · Competitors =
rivals also green but communicate poorly; the real rival is *indifference at the counter* · Collaborators
= SME sellers as broadcasters, staff, seller communities, owned channels (LINE/FB/IG/screens) · Context =
tightening green-marketing law + rising eco-awareness + green skepticism.

**Problem tree:** symptom (low awareness) → trunk (green is invisible at point of sale) → roots (no green
message on receipt/screen/staff; GoGreen Plus not surfaced to walk-ins; customers come for speed; legal
caution → under-communication).

**SWOT:** *S* — provable creds, a customer-usable product (GoGreen Plus), 83% warm audience, owned
touchpoints · *W* — story untold at point of sale, green isn't why they come · *O* — turn satisfaction
into pride, SME multiplier (free reach), specificity-as-trust · *T* — greenwashing/legal misstep, green
skepticism, attention scarcity, ฿5M/3mo limits.

**So what:** a winning idea must live at the **point of sale**, make the customer a **participant**, use a
**specific provable number in a human moment**, exploit the **SME multiplier**, fit the constraints, and
visibly respect **Green Claims**. → That's exactly what our system does (it's the situation's conclusion).

### 4. THE BIG IDEA + THE SYSTEM

#### Big Idea: "Proof That Travels" — campaign line **"Ship Love. Ship Light."** (ส่งความรัก ส่งอย่างรักษ์โลก)
We don't *tell* people DHL is green — we hand them **proof they personally helped**, and let it travel:
onto the box, across the border, onto a national map, into the seller's shop.

#### The 4 connected pieces — **Feel → Carry → Belong → Spread**
| Step | Piece | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| ① **Feel** | **Your Green Receipt** | "you funded a verified cut of X kg CO₂ ≈ Y km not driven" + QR (book-and-claim correct, GLEC method) |
| ② **Carry** | **Green Stamp** on the box | receiver *abroad* sees it → free cross-border reach |
| ③ **Belong** | **Thailand's Green Map** | localize DHL's real GoGreen Dashboard → collective national total |
| ④ **Spread** | **Seller Badge** | SMEs broadcast "Ships greener with DHL" → mirrors UOB's SME mission |

They plug together: the Receipt's data feeds the Map; the Stamp carries it abroad; the Badge makes sellers spread it.

#### The loop-closer: **Green Shipper tier** (connective tissue, not a 5th piece)
Status earned by **repeat green choices** (not raw spend): 🌱 Sprout → 🥉 Bronze → 🥈 Silver → 🥇 Gold.
The Receipt's running total = the tier progress bar; the Seller Badge levels up with the tier. This is what
makes the loop *repeat* (fixes the awareness→habit gap). Lives in LINE OA (near-zero build).
*Guardrails:* reward choices not spend · keep it a 10-sec pitch beat · behaviour-named (Green-Claims safe) · don't paywall the good feeling.

### 6. THE MONEY STORY & ฿5M BUDGET

**OpEx/CapEx?** No — a 3-month marketing spend is **OpEx by definition**; nothing's a capitalized asset
so CapEx is irrelevant (not even one business cycle). *Judo line:* "DHL already made the CapEx (€7B). Our
฿5M is awareness OpEx that activates an asset that already exists."

### 7. CARBON / SAF SCIENCE (defend it under fire)
- SAF = jet fuel from renewable/waste sources, **drop-in** (same planes, **same speed** — kills "green = slower").
- Costs **2–4×** fossil jet fuel; **under 1%** of global jet fuel supply → *that scarcity is why book-and-claim exists.*
- Cuts **up to ~80% lifecycle CO₂ on waste feedstocks** (crop ~55%) — claim the certified figure, never a blanket 80%.
- **The lifecycle truth:** SAF emits ~same CO₂ at the tailpipe; the saving is the carbon *came from today's
  air* (biogenic), not ancient oil. "Fossil = one-way street; SAF = near-closed loop. Leftover ~20–50% is the energy to make it."

---

### 8. GREEN-CLAIMS & METHODOLOGY (the winner-separator)
- **Book-and-claim:** a customer's parcel may NOT physically fly on SAF — DHL buys SAF somewhere, certifies
  the reduction. → Say **"you funded a verified reduction via SAF,"** NEVER "your parcel flew on SAF."
- **Cite GLEC Framework / ISO 14083** — the universal logistics-emissions standard, foundation of ISO 14083,
  **and DHL is a GLEC member.** Citing it = citing the standard DHL itself uses. Unbeatable credibility.
- **EmpCo** binding **27 Sep 2026** — bans generic claims + offset "carbon neutral." DHL's insetting survives it.
- **Never say:** "green/eco-friendly/sustainable" alone, "emission-free," "climate neutral/positive."
- **Always:** specific %, explain *how* (GLEC/SAF/insetting), provable. *If you can't prove it, don't say it.*
- Concrete example to show (label "illustrative" unless from MyDHL+): *"SAF 8% → cut ~340g → ≈ 3 km not driven (GLEC/ISO 14083)."*

### 🚨 0. THE BIG CATCH — our own SAF claim was wrong (fix it, then flaunt it)

GoGreen Plus runs on a **"book and claim"** system. When a customer pays for it, **their specific parcel
may NOT physically fly on SAF.** DHL buys SAF *somewhere* in its global network, an independent body
certifies the CO₂ reduction, and the customer receives a **certificate** for that reduction. The benefit
is deliberately *decoupled* from the physical fuel (because SAF isn't available everywhere).

**So the claim must change:**
- ❌ "Your shipment flew on SAF" — misleading; it might not have. *(This is the greenwashing the booklet warns against.)*
- ✅ **"You funded a verified cut of X kg CO₂ through sustainable aviation fuel in DHL's network"** — accurate, certificate-backed, provable.

➡️ **Turn the trap into the winning move:** showing the judges you understood book-and-claim and wrote the
claim *correctly* is the single clearest signal that you understood the brief, not just read it. Build the
"we respect Green Claims" story around this.

### ⛽ 3. THE SAF REALITY (so you can defend it under fire)

**What SAF is:** jet fuel from renewable/waste sources (used cooking oil, biomass, etc.), blended into
normal jet fuel, drop-in (no engine changes). Lower lifecycle CO₂ than fossil jet fuel.

**The honest caveats (a judge may push on these):**
- SAF is **~0.7% of global jet fuel** today; **2–3× more expensive** than fossil jet fuel until ~2030.
- IATA says the **10% SAF-by-2030 target is likely unreachable** (supply shortage).
- "SAF" is a **vague umbrella term** (biofuels → e-fuels vary wildly); a July 2025 NGO legal briefing warns
  overclaiming SAF carries legal risk.

**Your answer if challenged:** "We don't claim SAF solves aviation — we claim, specifically and provably,
that this shipment funded a *certified* X kg reduction. That precision is exactly why it's compliant."

### ⚖️ 4. THE REGULATORY TRAP, DEEPENED (and why DHL is on the safe side)

- The booklet's Appendix cites the **EU Empowering Consumers (EmpCo) Directive.** Status: the stricter
  *Green Claims Directive* was **paused/withdrawn in June 2025**, but **EmpCo is already law — binding from
  September 2026.**
- EmpCo will **ban generic green claims** ("eco-friendly," "sustainable") and **offset-based "climate
  neutral" claims.**
- **Why this favors DHL:** EmpCo cracks down on **offsetting** (buying reductions elsewhere). DHL uses
  **insetting** (reductions *inside* its own supply chain, via book-and-claim) — the more credible,
  **future-proof** approach. Pitch line: *"DHL's method is the one that survives the 2026 rules."*

**Claim hygiene (repeat in every asset):** specific kg + certificate + a "how" QR. Never "carbon neutral,"
"emission-free," "climate positive."

**Strategic implication (important):** Do **not** ask Thai customers to pay more or do more for green. The
winning frame is that they're **already helping, for free, just by shipping.** You remove the cost/effort
barrier that normally kills green behavior. The booklet's own 83%-satisfaction data means the warmth is
already there — you're attaching **pride** to an action they already take.

**Gen Z bonus:** Thai Gen Z purchase intent is driven by **social influence + brand image** → supports the
**shareable seller badge** and **#hashtag** mechanics for the student + SME personas.

### 🔬 6. BEHAVIORAL SCIENCE — name the mechanism (judges love this)

Each of our three ideas maps to an established **green-nudge**:
- **Green Receipt** = a **salience + feedback nudge** (making invisible impact visible & immediate — same

**Partnerships (low/no-cost, no celebrities)**
- Online seller communities / marketplaces (reach SMEs)

## ⭐ IDEA 1 — "Your Green Receipt" (ใบเสร็จสีเขียว)

#### The insight
Every shipper already receives a receipt. It's the one guaranteed touchpoint — and right now it
says nothing about the planet. Turn that ignored slip into **proof the customer just did something good.**

#### Core concept & slogan
- **Concept:** the receipt becomes a thank-you, not a transaction record.
- **Slogan (EN):** *"Proof you helped the planet."*
- **Slogan (TH):** *"ใบเสร็จที่ช่วยโลก"* / *"ส่งของวันนี้ ช่วยโลกไปด้วยกัน"*

#### The mechanic (exactly what happens)
1. Customer ships at a ServicePoint (esp. via GoGreen Plus / SAF).
2. Receipt (physical + digital via LINE/email) prints a **specific line**:
   *"คุณช่วยลด CO₂ ไป X กก. ผ่านเชื้อเพลิงการบินยั่งยืน (SAF) ในเครือข่ายของ DHL"*
   ("You funded a verified cut of X kg CO₂ via sustainable aviation fuel in DHL's network.")
   ⚠️ NOT "บินด้วย SAF / flew on SAF" — GoGreen Plus is **book-and-claim**; the parcel may not physically
   fly on SAF, the customer funds a *certified* reduction. (See [research](DHL-RESEARCH-deepdive.md) §0.)
3. A **QR code** → a page explaining *how* the number is calculated (SAF, insetting, book-and-claim).
   This is the Green-Claims shield: specific + provable, not vague.
4. **Carbon → tangible**: show the number as something feel-able ("≈ X km not driven").
5. **Running total** for repeat shippers: "You've helped cut ___ kg CO₂ with DHL."

#### Customer journey (touchpoints)
walk in → staff one-liner ("this one helped cut CO₂!") → ship → receive Green Receipt →
scan QR (sees the *how*) → keeps/screenshots → digital copy lands in LINE → returns, total grows.

#### Budget sketch (within ฿5M / 3 mo)
- Receipt + in-branch screen redesign & rollout to 36 branches
- QR landing page + LINE OA integration (digital receipt + running total)
- Staff briefing & one-liner script (near-free, high impact)
- Pre/post awareness survey at ServicePoints (mirrors the case's own survey method)
- Light social amplification of real customer receipts (#)

#### KPIs
QR scan rate · LINE digital-receipt opens · awareness lift (pre vs post survey) ·
GoGreen Plus uptake at counter · repeat green-shipping rate · cost per aware customer (ROI).

#### Why it wins (map to judging)
Creativity: boring receipt → thank-you moment · Insight: hits the Key Question at point of action ·
Feasibility: existing receipts/screens/LINE, no new tech, no celebs · Impact: clean measurable funnel ·
Storytelling: "the proof is in your hands."

#### Tough-question prep
- *"A receipt gets thrown away."* → It's also digital (LINE/email) + the in-store screen + staff line.
  The paper is one of several touchpoints; the digital copy + running total persist.
- *"Is the kg number credible / not greenwashing?"* → QR shows the methodology; fully Green-Claims-aligned
  (specific, sourced, never "carbon neutral").
- *"Awareness vs. behavior?"* → the running total + repeat loop + (Idea 2) badge drive repeat & advocacy.

#### Customer journey
seller ships green → badge unlocked (1-tap in LINE) → adds to shop/socials/packaging →

#### Budget sketch

#### KPIs

#### Why it wins
Creativity: makes the customer a *broadcaster* · Insight: targets the highest-leverage persona ·
Feasibility: digital assets + existing LINE, scales itself · Impact: earned reach = huge ROI ·
Storytelling: "your shop, greener."

### 0. THE CREATIVE UNLOCK — "international" isn't a constraint, it's the gift

This is **cross-border express shipping**. Every parcel literally *crosses borders.* So the green story can too.
Most teams will pitch a local awareness campaign. The winning creative move: **let the impact travel with
the parcel.**

> **Core creative idea:** *"Your kindness to the planet crosses borders with your parcel."*
> The sender (Thailand) ships green → a mark on the box → the **receiver abroad** sees it → DHL's green story
> reaches another country **for free.** One ฿36 shipment buys cross-border earned media.

That single reframe makes the campaign **international by design**, not by budget.

|---|---|---|
| **Renault "Cars to Work"** (Cannes 2024 SDG Grand Prix) | Didn't *advertise* mobility — *solved* a real barrier (free cars to reach job interviews) | Don't advertise "DHL is green" — **remove the barrier**: show customers they're *already* helping, free. Action > slogan. |
| **Natura "Amazon Greenventory"** (Cannes 2025 SDG) | Made the invisible visible — inventoried Amazon biodiversity into a usable asset | **Inventory the invisible CO₂** each customer cuts → the **Impact Counter**. Turn unseen reductions into a visible, shareable number. |

| **HubSpot / IBM Partner Plus / Hyatt** (B2B2C badge programs) | Partners earn shareable badges/tiers + a live dashboard → become advocates | Validates the **Seller Badge** — SMEs earn "Ships greener with DHL," share it, become your channel. Proven model. |

### 3. THE CARBON LIFECYCLE — where the CO₂ actually goes (the part people miss)

**Key truth most people get wrong:** SAF emits **roughly the SAME CO₂ out the back of the plane** as normal
jet fuel when burned. The saving is **not** at the tailpipe — it's in **where the carbon came from.** This is
why it's measured "**well-to-wake**" (the whole life: feedstock → production → transport → combustion), not
just combustion.

#### Fossil jet fuel = a ONE-WAY street (carbon added)
```
Ancient plants (millions of yrs ago) → buried → crude oil (carbon locked underground)
   → refine → burn in plane → CO₂ to atmosphere
   = brand-NEW carbon added to the air that was safely locked away. Atmosphere CO₂ ↑.
```

#### SAF = a (near) CLOSED LOOP (carbon borrowed & returned)
```
Plants/crops grow NOW → absorb CO₂ from today's air → become waste oil / biomass
   → collect → refine into SAF → burn in plane → CO₂ back to atmosphere
   = returning carbon that was JUST taken out. Roughly circular.
   Net add ≈ only the fossil energy used to farm/collect/refine/ship it.
```

#### Why the number is "~50–80% lower," not "zero"
- The **carbon itself** is biogenic (recently from the air), so for accounting its **tank-to-wake factor ≈ 0**.
- But making and moving SAF still uses some energy → that's the **~20–50% that remains.**
- **Waste feedstocks** (used cooking oil) score best (~76–97% cut) because the feedstock is "free" carbon already
  destined for disposal; **crop-based** scores worse (soy ~55%) because farming has its own footprint.

> **One-line explainer for a judge:** *"SAF doesn't burn cleaner at the tailpipe — it recycles carbon. Fossil
> fuel digs up ancient carbon and adds it to the sky; SAF burns carbon that plants pulled from today's air, so
> the loop nearly closes. The leftover ~20–50% is just the energy used to make it."*

---

### 4. WHY THIS MATTERS FOR "SHIP LOVE. SHIP LIGHT."

- **The surcharge is justified, not a markup:** when a customer pays a little extra for GoGreen Plus, they're
  covering a fuel that genuinely costs 2–4× more — *"your few baht buys the expensive, real stuff."*
- **The drop-in fact protects DHL's brand promise:** speed and reliability are unchanged. *Same fast DHL, lighter footprint.*
- **The supply reality explains book-and-claim** (the heart of your Green-Claims credibility): you can't promise
  *this* parcel flew on SAF, so you promise a *verified reduction* instead. Accurate by design.
- **Co-benefits = a deeper emotional hook:** "you didn't just cut carbon — you helped clear the air over a city."

> ⚠️ Pitch-safe phrasing: *"SAF is cleaner jet fuel — costs 2–4× more, cuts up to ~80% of lifecycle CO₂ on

> (book-and-claim) so your contribution is real and provable."*

  no multi-year payback. **3 months isn't even one business cycle**, so a CapEx/OpEx framework is the
  wrong tool (and over-engineering it can signal you misread the brief).
- ✅ **Right tool instead:** simple **campaign budget allocation + cost-effectiveness + ROI** — which is
  exactly what the booklet asks for ("cost-effectiveness," p3; budget allocation, p3/p14).

**The judo move (say this if a judge probes investment):**
> *"DHL already made the CapEx — €7B in green capability they've already built. Our ฿5M is pure
> marketing OpEx that *activates awareness* of an asset that already exists. We don't spend on carbon —
> customers fund that through GoGreen Plus. We spend only on awareness."*
> → This *uses* the OpEx/CapEx distinction to show literacy, and pins the big investment on DHL, not you.

---

### 1. THE TOP-LINE SPLIT (฿5,000,000)

Guardrail: ฿5M ÷ 36 branches ÷ 3 months ≈ **฿46,300 / branch / month.** Favour **cheap, repeating**
touchpoints (receipt, screen, staff line, LINE) over one-off spectacle. No celebrities (excluded by brief).

| # | Bucket | % | ฿ | What it buys |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **In-branch experience** (36 ServicePoints) | 30% | **1,500,000** | Receipt redesign + printing, counter cards, posters, screen content for all 36 branches |
| 2 | **Digital build** (Receipt QR, LINE OA, the Map) | 20% | **1,000,000** | QR landing page, LINE OA integration (digital receipt + running total), localized GoGreen Map |
| 3 | **Seller Badge + SME activation** | 18% | **900,000** | Badge system + asset kit, outreach to seller communities/marketplaces |
| 4 | **Content & social** (video + #ShipLight UGC) | 15% | **750,000** | The real-SME ServicePoint video, social content, organic amplification |
| 5 | **Staff enablement** | 5% | **250,000** | Briefing + the one-liner script across 36 branches (cheap, high-impact) |
| 6 | **Measurement** (pre/post awareness survey) | 7% | **350,000** | Baseline + post survey (mirrors the booklet's own 83% method) → proves ROI |
| 7 | **Contingency** | 5% | **250,000** | Buffer (always reserve ~5%) |
| | **TOTAL** | **100%** | **5,000,000** | |

> These are **planning percentages** for the pitch, not quotes — label them "indicative allocation."
> Judges want to see *sound logic and a contingency*, not invoice-level precision.

---

### 2. PHASED OVER 3 MONTHS

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Rough spend |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Build & baseline** | 1–3 | Design receipts/stamp/QR, build LINE + Map, staff script, **baseline survey** | setup-heavy (~30%) |
| **2 · Launch at the counter** | 4–8 | Go live across 36 branches; screens/posters/cards; LINE push; seed the Map | rollout-heavy (~45%) |
| **3 · Spread & prove** | 9–12 | Seller Badge to SME communities; #ShipLight UGC; milestones; **post survey + ROI readout** | amplify + measure (~20%) + contingency |

---

### 3. COST-EFFECTIVENESS / ROI (what the ฿5M *returns*)

**The ฿5M buys awareness; awareness manufactures margin (it doesn't pay for carbon — customers do).**

- **Cost per aware customer** = ฿5M ÷ (customers reached × awareness-lift %). Track via pre/post survey.
- **Engagement efficiency** = cost per QR scan · per LINE open · per badge claim.
- **Behavioural return** = lift in GoGreen Plus opt-ins (the high-margin revenue line for DHL).
- **Earned return** = Stamp impressions abroad + seller-audience reach (free, multiplies the ฿5M).
- **Headline ROI line:** *"For ฿5M of awareness OpEx, DHL activates a green capability it already paid
  €7B to build, lifts a high-margin product (GoGreen Plus), and turns SME customers into free
  broadcasters — measured by an awareness survey before and after."*

---

### 4. WHY THIS BUDGET WINS THE "FEASIBILITY" + "IMPACT/ROI" CRITERIA (40% of score)
- **Feasibility:** every bucket reuses existing assets (branches, LINE, receipts, staff) — buildable in
  3 months, no celebrities, within ฿5M. Has a contingency.
- **Impact/ROI:** explicit cost-effectiveness metrics + a measurement bucket that *proves* the lift.
- **Discipline:** it's a marketing budget treated as a marketing budget — not over-modelled. That itself
  signals you understood the brief.

## 19. 🏅 DHL Challenge — The Green Shipper Tier (loyalty / status)

*Source: `DHL-TIER-loyalty.md`*

## 🏅 DHL Challenge — The Green Shipper Tier (loyalty / status)

> Pakkapon's "customer tier" idea, built to **close the flywheel loop.** It's not a 5th piece — it's the
> **connective tissue** that makes Feel→Carry→Belong→Spread actually *repeat.*
> Companions: [master strategy](DHL-MASTER-strategy.md) · [money story](DHL-MARGIN-money-story.md) ·
> [team feedback](DHL-TEAM-feedback.md).

---

### 0. WHY IT FITS (the honest case)

The flywheel's weakest link is **"comes back again"** — awareness is easy, but *repeat* green behaviour is
hard (the whole value–action gap). A status tier is **literally a repeat-engagement engine**, and it
plugs straight into two things we already have:
- The **running total** on the Green Receipt → *becomes* the tier progress bar.
- The **Seller Badge** → higher tiers = a more prestigious badge (more valuable to the SME).

So the tier doesn't add a new mechanic — it **upgrades the loop** between the pieces we built.

> **One line:** *The receipt shows your impact; the tier turns that impact into status; the status makes
> you ship green again. That's the loop closing.*

---

### 1. THE MECHANIC — "Green Shipper" status

Earned by **repeat green choices** (number of GoGreen Plus shipments / verified CO₂ cut over time),
**not by raw spend.** (Critical — see the guardrail.)

| Tier | Earned by | Reward (escalating, mostly free) |
|---|---|---|
| 🌱 **Sprout** | 1st green shipment | Welcome + your first Green Receipt + "you've started" |
| 🥉 **Bronze** | a few green shipments | Basic "Green Shipper" badge for your shop/socials |
| 🥈 **Silver** | steady repeat | Better badge tier + featured on the Green Map |
| 🥇 **Gold** | power green-shipper | Premium "Gold Green Shipper" badge + a spotlight story (the SME video pipeline) |

- Lives in **LINE OA** (where the digital receipt + running total already are) → near-zero build cost.
- Progress is **visible** ("2 more shipments to Silver") → the salience nudge that drives return.

---

### 2. HOW IT THREADS THE EXISTING 4 PIECES (connective tissue, not a 5th mode)
```
① FEEL  — Green Receipt shows impact  ──► feeds your tier progress bar
② CARRY — Green Stamp on the box      ──► higher tier = a nicer/branded stamp
③ BELONG— Thailand's Green Map        ──► Silver+ shippers featured on the map
④ SPREAD— Seller Badge                ──► the badge LEVELS UP with your tier
            ▲                                        │
            └──────────  status makes you ship green AGAIN  ◄──────────┘
```
The tier is the **arrow that returns** — it's what turns a one-time good feeling into a habit.

---

### 3. WHY IT STRENGTHENS THE MICRO-SME SPINE
- A small home seller earning **"Gold Green Shipper"** is *exactly* the empowerment-of-the-little-guy
  story you locked in. Status is something SMEs can **show their own customers** → ties to the multiplier.
- It mirrors **UOB** again: tiers/levels are how banks build SME loyalty programs. Familiar, credible.
- It rewards the behaviour DHL *wants* (repeat GoGreen Plus) without asking anyone to "pay more for green."

---

### 4. ⚠️ GUARDRAILS (so it helps, not hurts)
1. **Reward repeat green CHOICES, not raw spend.** "Ship green often," never "spend more to rank up" —
   the latter clashes with the "you're already helping, for free" frame and a bank judge will smell it.
2. **Keep it connective, not a 5th pillar.** In the pitch it's *"and the tier makes the loop repeat,"*
   a 10-second beat — not its own mode competing with the 4 pieces. (Resists scope creep.)
3. **Green-Claims safe.** Tier names are about *behaviour* ("Green Shipper"), not unprovable outcome
   claims ("carbon-neutral customer"). Status reflects verified GoGreen Plus usage (book-and-claim).
4. **Don't gate the good feeling behind a tier.** Tier 0 (anyone who ships green once) already gets the
   Receipt + the feeling; tiers add *status on top*, they don't paywall participation.

---

### 5. ONE-LINE FOR THE PITCH
> *"The Green Receipt makes you feel it once. The Green Shipper tier makes you want to feel it again —
> turning a single good choice into a status you carry, show, and grow. That's how awareness becomes habit."*

## 20. 🏛️ Atlas · Case Project — Architecture

## 🏛️ Atlas · Case Project — Architecture

How the case-study side of Atlas is organised. The goal: **one source of truth in Markdown**, a
**clean rendered view** on the live site, and a **fact-integrity discipline** so nothing fake reaches a pitch.

---

### 1. The two layers (data flow)

```
  ┌─────────────────────┐   the original brief (PDF, 18 pages)
  │   SOURCE BRIEF      │   DHL Express Sustainability Case Booklet
  └──────────┬──────────┘
             │  read · extract · tag every fact with its page (pX)
             ▼
  ┌─────────────────────┐   library/cases/*.md
  │   WORKING LAYER     │   raw editable notes — the SOURCE OF TRUTH.
  │   (Markdown)        │   travels with `git clone`, diff-able, team-editable.
  └──────────┬──────────┘
             │  hand-render the highlights into HTML (curated, not auto-generated)
             ▼
  ┌─────────────────────┐   library/cases.html
  │   PRESENTATION      │   the published, styled view on the Atlas PWA.
  │   (cases.html)      │   accordion (<details>) per section. Links back to the .md.
  └──────────┬──────────┘
             │  git push → GitHub Pages (main branch root)
             ▼
        oksoimcodingnow.github.io/atlas/library/cases.html   (LIVE)
```

**Rule:** edit the **Markdown first**, then mirror the change into `cases.html`. The `.md` is canonical;
the HTML is a curated highlight reel. They must never contradict each other.

---

### 2. The document set (per case) — and what each is for

| Layer | File | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | `CASE-summary.md` (`DHL-Express-UOB-WonderLab-2026.md`) | Canonical facts + **page map**. The index everything cites. |
| Quick | `CASE-CHEAT-SHEET.md` | One-glance pre-meeting sheet. |
| Analysis | `CASE-SITUATION-analysis.md` | 5C / SWOT / problem-tree / funnel. The "why." |
| Ideas | `CASE-STRATEGY-bank.md` | Breadth — every angle + build-outs. |
| Ideas | `CASE-TOP3-deepdive.md` | Depth — the strongest few, fully built. |
| Ideas | `CASE-IDEAS-brainstorm.md` | Live working scratchpad. |
| Evidence | `CASE-RESEARCH-deepdive.md` | External research (competitors, regulation, sources). |
| Evidence | `CASE-CARBON-ECONOMICS.md` | The numbers / cost model / ROI. |
| Meta | `README.md` · `ARCHITECTURE.md` | Index + this doc. |

**Naming:** `CASENAME-TOPIC.md`, kebab/caps topic. New case = new prefix.

**Dependency direction:** analysis & ideas → cite → **summary** (facts) and **research/economics** (evidence).
Facts live in *one* place (the summary's page map); everything else references them. Don't duplicate a number
across files — link to where it's defined.

---

### 3. Fact-integrity discipline (the part that wins or loses pitches)

Three tiers of truth, always visibly labelled:

1. **Booklet fact** → tag the page: `(p14)`. Verifiable in the source brief.
2. **External research** → cite a source link. Lives in research/economics docs.
3. **Assumption / our idea** → flag explicitly: `⚠️ ASSUMED — not in booklet` or `(our working line)`.

Hard rules:
- **Never present an assumption as a booklet fact.** (We caught & fixed: an invented challenge tagline,
  fake prize splits, an unconfirmed pitch date, assumed judging criteria.)
- **Claim hygiene for green statements:** specific number + method + source; the GoGreen Plus claim is
  **book-and-claim** → "funded a verified reduction," never "flew on SAF." Never "carbon neutral."
- **Illustrative numbers are labelled illustrative** and list what real figure must replace them (e.g.
  MyDHL+ for per-shipment kg).

---

### 4. How `cases.html` is built (conventions)

- Self-contained: inline `<style>`, design tokens in `:root`, DHL brand vars (`--dhl-yellow/red`).
- One **`<div class="case">`** per case → `.case-head` (badge/title/status) + a stack of **`<details>`**
  accordions, one per `.md` doc.
- Reusable blocks: `.key` (highlight), `.num-grid`/`.stat` (numbers), `.body table` (compare),
  `.good`/`.bad` (do/don't), `.src` (source/footnote line linking the raw `.md`).
- Shared FX script: `../lib/atlas-fx.js`. Manifest/icons from atlas root (it's a PWA).

**To add a new case:** (1) write the `.md` docs, (2) list them in `README.md`, (3) clone a `.case` block in
`cases.html` and fill the accordions, (4) add a row/link on `library/index.html` if needed, (5) commit + push.

---

### 5. Deploy

- **Host:** GitHub Pages, served from **`main` branch root** (no build step, no workflow).
- **Live URL:** `https://oksoimcodingnow.github.io/atlas/library/cases.html`
- **Publish = `git push origin main`.** Pages redeploys automatically in ~1 min. Because there's no build,
  **whatever is in `main` is live** — so the fact-integrity rules above matter: a wrong claim pushed is a
  wrong claim published.

---

### 6. Roadmap (nice-to-haves)
- [ ] Interactive **ROI calculator** (HTML): inputs customers/day, opt-in %, avg kg → CO₂ cut + cost/aware-customer.
- [ ] A small **Markdown→HTML** helper so the rendered view can't drift from the notes.
- [ ] Per-case **status badge** (Active / Submitted / Won) driven from `README.md`.

  estimate is methodologically legitimate, not made up.

**Pitch wording (safe + rigorous):**
> *"Every figure on the receipt is calculated with the **GLEC Framework (ISO 14083)** — the industry-standard
> logistics-emissions method DHL itself uses — and the reduction is certified via book-and-claim. Specific,
> standardized, provable."*

> ⚠️ Keep the SAF wording correct: *"you funded a verified reduction via SAF (GLEC/ISO 14083, book-and-claim),"*
