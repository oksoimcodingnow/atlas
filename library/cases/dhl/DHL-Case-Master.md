## 1. THE CAMPAIGN IN ONE PAGE
**North star:** *Make DHL's invisible green work visible, at the moment of shipping, and let the proof travel — so customers know, feel, and spread that they helped.*

- **Problem:** 83% love DHL, almost none know it's green. DHL does real green work; at the counter it's invisible.
- **Insight:** Thais care (97%) but don't act (value-action gap). So don't ask them to pay/do more — show they're *already helping, for free*.
- **Big Idea:** **"Proof That Travels" — Ship Love. Ship Light.** Hand customers proof they helped; let it ride the parcel across borders.
- **System:** Feel → Carry → Belong → Spread, closed into a loop by the Green Shipper Tier.
- **Deliverable:** awareness lift (pre/post survey), measured the way DHL got its own 83%.

## 2. THE BASICS (context, grouped)
*(The situation, research, and competitive context — condensed. Full versions: [situation](DHL-SITUATION-analysis.md) · [research](DHL-RESEARCH-deepdive.md) · [round 2](DHL-RESEARCH-round2.md) · [benchmark](DHL-BENCHMARK-awareness.md).)*

- **The gap:** high satisfaction (83%), near-zero sustainability awareness. The campaign closes this.
- **The consumer:** 97% climate-concerned, ~11% APAC willing-to-pay premium, but economic worry blocks action. Gen Z driven by social proof + authenticity (punishes greenwashing).
- **The moat:** in Thailand DHL already leads — **43 EVs vs FedEx's 12**, GoGreen Plus is the only customer-usable green product at the counter, DHL is a **top-3 global SAF buyer**.
- **The product (exists, we don't invent it):** GoGreen Plus — pay to cut shipment CO₂ via SAF, **book-and-claim** (verified reduction, not "your parcel flew on SAF"). ~€325/tonne. Already 12,000+ APAC users incl. Kasikornbank, Google.
- **The science (one line):** SAF = drop-in fuel from waste/renewables; cuts up to ~80% on waste feedstock; verified via ISCC Credit Transfer. *(Depth: [SAF](DHL-SAF-vs-jetfuel.md) · [carbon](DHL-CARBON-ECONOMICS.md) · [ecosystem](DHL-ECOSYSTEM.md).)*

## 3. THE STRATEGY — PROOF THAT TRAVELS
*(Full: [master strategy](DHL-MASTER-strategy.md). The 4 pieces form one repeating loop.)*

| Piece | Mechanic | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| ① **FEEL** | Green Receipt | "You funded a verified cut of X kg CO₂ ≈ ~6 km/kg not driven" — proof in hand at the counter |
| ② **CARRY** | Green Stamp | a mark on the box; the receiver abroad sees it → free cross-border reach |
| ③ **BELONG** | Green Map | localized GoGreen Dashboard → a national total ticking up across 36 branches |
| ④ **SPREAD** | Seller Badge | SME sellers unlock a shareable badge → broadcast to their own buyers (B2B2C, mirrors UOB's Compass) |

🏅 **The Green Shipper Tier** closes the loop — status makes you ship green again. Ranked by **greenness, not size** (a 40-parcel SME can hit Gold while an 8,000-parcel firm sits at Bronze). *(Math: [tier model](DHL-TIER-MODEL.md).)*

## 6. WHY WE WIN
1. **Green-Claims mastery:** book-and-claim precision → survives EmpCo 2026 + Thai Consumer Protection Act. Compliant by design (LINE opt-in under PDPA). *(Full: [policy risk](DHL-POLICY-RISK.md).)*
2. **The UOB mirror:** the Compass starts the SME journey; Ship Light lets them *prove* it.
3. **Proof it works:** Patagonia +30%, Lavazza brand-lift 20×, IKEA 75% — honesty-led green campaigns measurably move people.
4. **Feasibility:** reuses DHL's real assets; no new tech, no celebrities; inside ฿5M.

**Close:** *"X tonnes cut — and a country that knows it shipped them. Ship Love. Ship Light."*
