Appendix A2 · Tier formula

  • "Small" = DHL's own SME/ServicePoint account flag, aligned to Thailand's OSMEP definition (≤฿300M / 100 staff).

Appendix A4 · Book-and-claim

Why our SAF claim is compliant, not greenwashing.

  • GoGreen Plus is book-and-claim: the parcel may not physically fly on SAF.
  • DHL buys certified SAF in its network; the customer gets a verified reduction certificate.
  • We say "you funded a verified reduction," never "your parcel flew on SAF." Insetting, not offsetting.
