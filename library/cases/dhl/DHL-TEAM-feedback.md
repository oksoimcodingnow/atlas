## ✅ Verified: cite the **GLEC Framework** (this is the winning citation)
- **GLEC** = Global Logistics Emissions Council Framework (by Smart Freight Centre) — *the* universal
  method for calculating & reporting freight-transport emissions, the whole chain (consignor → consignee).
- It is the **foundation of ISO 14083:2023**, the official global standard for transport-chain emissions.
- **DHL is a GLEC member.** → Citing GLEC means citing the standard **DHL itself uses.** Unbeatable for credibility.
- GLEC has data-precision *levels* (basic shipment details → actual vehicle data) — so a per-parcel
  estimate is methodologically legitimate, not made up.

**Pitch wording (safe + rigorous):**
> *"Every figure on the receipt is calculated with the **GLEC Framework (ISO 14083)** — the industry-standard
> logistics-emissions method DHL itself uses — and the reduction is certified via book-and-claim. Specific,
> standardized, provable."*

> ⚠️ Keep the SAF wording correct: *"you funded a verified reduction via SAF (GLEC/ISO 14083, book-and-claim),"*
> not "your parcel flew on SAF." Numbers like "SAF 8% → 340g → 3 km" are great **as an illustrative example** —
> label them as such unless pulled from a real DHL/GLEC calc.

## Feedback round 2 — Pakkapon's brain-dump (24 Jun 2026)

> Lots of ideas at once. Captured faithfully, then triaged. **Guiding direction (locked):
> the whole campaign leans toward supporting Micro-SMEs & small customers** — "fairness for
> the little guys." This is the spine everything else folds into.

### A. Campaign = a *system of multiple modes*, not one tactic
- Points/loyalty, a QR **carbon-footprint journey**, etc. At the ServicePoint the QR lets the
  customer *see/track* the shipment's carbon journey.
- ✅ Good instinct, **but** keep it focused — fold the best into the existing 4-piece spine
  (Feel→Carry→Belong→Spread), don't bolt on 5 separate modes (scope-creep risk).

### B. QR = "carbon trace, traceable as much as possible" ⭐
- The box's sources/journey + receipt + **impact in a concrete carbon unit.**
- His economic point: the **abatement cost is already paid by customers** who use the service →
  DHL earns green credit "for free." (Ties straight to our money story — good.)

### C. SME social badge — with an honest risk flag ⚠️
- Free incentive sellers share — **but** "no different if they already choose eco / already pay more."
  Will it actually move them? → make it genuinely *useful* (a sales asset), per our money-story logic.
- He stresses the **physical** story too (biodegradable / what happens to the box).
  ⚠️ **Caution:** DHL's green story is **shipping carbon (SAF)**, not packaging. Don't claim
  biodegradable packaging unless DHL actually does it — Green-Claims trap. Verify before using.

### D. The big creative reframe — "MULTIPLE MODES" + **SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities)** 🎯
- Wartime/historical letters; the **Louis Vuitton** angle: even a small home seller can ship
  worldwide, like LV — **shipping is an equalizer.** "Fairness for the little guys in this country."
- ✅ **This is the freshest hook in the whole case.** Elevates "Ship Love. Ship Light." from
  *carbon* → *carbon + fairness/opportunity for small businesses.* Judges won't have heard it 10×.

### E. Persona + format (locked)
- **Primary persona: Micro-SME / online sellers running e-commerce from home** (+ small customers).
- Tone & channels follow from this. **Format idea:** a ServicePoint **video** telling a *real* SME's
  story — used DHL to ship abroad, opened new markets they couldn't reach before — and that shipment
  was **carbon-equivalent to SAF/EV.** Emotional + concrete + on-brand.

### ✅ How this sharpens the strategy
- **Reframe the spine around small-business empowerment:** "DHL + the little guy, shipping greener
  and reaching the world." Carbon is the *proof*; **opportunity/fairness (SDG 10)** is the *emotion*.
- The **Seller Badge** becomes the hero piece (it's literally the SME's tool), not just one of four.
- **Mirrors UOB** even harder: a bank that empowers SMEs → a campaign that empowers SMEs. Perfect fit.
