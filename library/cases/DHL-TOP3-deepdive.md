# 🏆 DHL Challenge — Top 3 Ideas, Deep Dive

> The strongest spine, each fully built. They reinforce each other → you can pitch one,
> or all three as a connected system. Anchored to: [strategy bank](DHL-STRATEGY-bank.md) ·
> [summary](DHL-Express-UOB-WonderLab-2026.md).
> Constraints throughout: **฿5M · 36 branches · 3 months · no celebrities · Green-Claims-compliant.**

**The connecting thesis (say this in the pitch):**
> DHL's green is real but invisible at the moment it happens. We make it *felt* (Green Receipt),
> *spread* it through the people who ship most (Seller Badge), and make it *collective and proud*
> (Impact Counter). One system: feel → share → belong.

# ⭐ IDEA 1 — "Your Green Receipt" (ใบเสร็จสีเขียว)

### The insight
Every shipper already receives a receipt. It's the one guaranteed touchpoint — and right now it
says nothing about the planet. Turn that ignored slip into **proof the customer just did something good.**

### Core concept & slogan
- **Concept:** the receipt becomes a thank-you, not a transaction record.
- **Slogan (EN):** *"Proof you helped the planet."*
- **Slogan (TH):** *"ใบเสร็จที่ช่วยโลก"* / *"ส่งของวันนี้ ช่วยโลกไปด้วยกัน"*

### The mechanic (exactly what happens)
1. Customer ships at a ServicePoint (esp. via GoGreen Plus / SAF).
2. Receipt (physical + digital via LINE/email) prints a **specific line**:
   *"คุณช่วยลด CO₂ ไป X กก. ผ่านเชื้อเพลิงการบินยั่งยืน (SAF) ในเครือข่ายของ DHL"*
   ("You funded a verified cut of X kg CO₂ via sustainable aviation fuel in DHL's network.")
   ⚠️ NOT "บินด้วย SAF / flew on SAF" — GoGreen Plus is **book-and-claim**; the parcel may not physically
   fly on SAF, the customer funds a *certified* reduction. (See [research](DHL-RESEARCH-deepdive.md) §0.)
3. A **QR code** → a page explaining *how* the number is calculated (SAF, insetting, book-and-claim).
   This is the Green-Claims shield: specific + provable, not vague.
4. **Carbon → tangible**: show the number as something feel-able ("≈ X km not driven").
5. **Running total** for repeat shippers: "You've helped cut ___ kg CO₂ with DHL."

### Customer journey (touchpoints)
walk in → staff one-liner ("this one helped cut CO₂!") → ship → receive Green Receipt →
scan QR (sees the *how*) → keeps/screenshots → digital copy lands in LINE → returns, total grows.

### Budget sketch (within ฿5M / 3 mo)
- Receipt + in-branch screen redesign & rollout to 36 branches
- QR landing page + LINE OA integration (digital receipt + running total)
- Staff briefing & one-liner script (near-free, high impact)
- Pre/post awareness survey at ServicePoints (mirrors the case's own survey method)
- Light social amplification of real customer receipts (#)

### KPIs
QR scan rate · LINE digital-receipt opens · awareness lift (pre vs post survey) ·
GoGreen Plus uptake at counter · repeat green-shipping rate · cost per aware customer (ROI).

### Why it wins (map to judging)
Creativity: boring receipt → thank-you moment · Insight: hits the Key Question at point of action ·
Feasibility: existing receipts/screens/LINE, no new tech, no celebs · Impact: clean measurable funnel ·
Storytelling: "the proof is in your hands."

### Tough-question prep
- *"A receipt gets thrown away."* → It's also digital (LINE/email) + the in-store screen + staff line.
  The paper is one of several touchpoints; the digital copy + running total persist.
- *"Is the kg number credible / not greenwashing?"* → QR shows the methodology; fully Green-Claims-aligned
  (specific, sourced, never "carbon neutral").
- *"Awareness vs. behavior?"* → the running total + repeat loop + (Idea 2) badge drive repeat & advocacy.

# ⭐ IDEA 2 — "Ships Greener with DHL" Seller Badge (the multiplier)

### The insight (why this is the smartest ROI play)
SME / online sellers are **frequent shippers** AND they have **their own audiences**. Give them a badge
and one DHL message reaches the seller *and* every buyer the seller talks to. It's B2B2C leverage —
earned reach DHL doesn't pay for. And sellers *want* it, because it helps them sell.

### Core concept & slogan
- **Concept:** turn DHL's sustainability into the *seller's* selling point.
- **Slogan (EN):** *"Sell it. Ship it greener."*
- **Slogan (TH):** *"ร้านนี้ส่งของแบบรักษ์โลกกับ DHL"*

### The mechanic
1. SME ships via GoGreen Plus → unlocks a **verified digital badge** in LINE OA.
2. Badge kit: IG/Facebook sticker, shop banner, packaging insert, "as seen at checkout" line.
3. Seller displays it to their customers → buyers see "this shop ships greener" → preference + trust.
4. Badge links to what it actually means (GoGreen Plus + method) — keeps it honest.

### Customer journey
seller ships green → badge unlocked (1-tap in LINE) → adds to shop/socials/packaging →
buyers notice → buyers prefer green seller → seller ships more green with DHL → repeat.

### Budget sketch
- Badge design + verification system (tie to GoGreen Plus usage) via LINE OA
- Asset kit (stickers, banners, inserts) — digital, near-zero marginal cost
- Outreach to seller communities / marketplaces (no celebs; community partnerships)
- Tracking dashboard for claims & reach

### KPIs
Badge claims · seller-audience reach (earned impressions) · repeat green-shipping volume per seller ·
new customers attributed to green positioning (seller survey) · ROI vs. near-zero asset cost.

### Why it wins
Creativity: makes the customer a *broadcaster* · Insight: targets the highest-leverage persona ·
Feasibility: digital assets + existing LINE, scales itself · Impact: earned reach = huge ROI ·
Storytelling: "your shop, greener."

### Tough-question prep
- *"Will sellers bother?"* → It's a sales asset, not a chore; 1-tap to claim; tied to a real buyer-trust benefit.
- *"Greenwashing risk if a seller overclaims?"* → Badge wording is controlled, links to method, scoped to
  the shipments actually sent green (not the seller's whole business).

# ⭐ IDEA 3 — "The Impact Counter" (collective pride)

### The insight
Individual impact feels tiny ("my one parcel?"). A **shared, visible, growing total** makes it feel real
and makes people want to be part of it — and to come back to add to it.

### Core concept & slogan
- **Concept:** turn private green acts into a public, collective win.
- **Slogan (EN):** *"Look what we cut, together."*
- **Slogan (TH):** *"รวมกันเราลด CO₂ ได้เท่านี้"*

### The mechanic
1. A **live tally** on each branch's screen + an online dashboard:
   *"ลูกค้าที่สาขานี้ช่วยลด CO₂ ไปแล้ว ___ กก. เดือนนี้."*
2. Fed by the Green Receipt data (Idea 1) → the three ideas literally plug together.
3. **Carbon → tangible** at scale ("= X trees for a year") so the collective number lands.
4. Optional milestones / a national total across all 36 branches.

### Customer journey
sees the counter on arrival → ships → "did mine count?" → checks receipt/online → sees total tick up →
feels part of something → returns, shares the screenshot.

### Budget sketch
- Dashboard build (branch screens + web), fed by existing GoGreen Plus / receipt data
- Branch screen content rollout (36)
- Honest data pipeline + sourcing (Green Claims)
- Social moments around milestones

### KPIs
Screen dwell/engagement · online dashboard visits · milestone share rate · correlation with repeat
shipping · awareness lift contribution.

### Why it wins
Creativity: a city-wide live scoreboard for good · Insight: solves "my impact is tiny" ·
Feasibility: reuses receipt data + existing screens · Impact: visible, measurable, shareable ·
Storytelling: "together."

### Tough-question prep
- *"Where does the data come from?"* → real GoGreen Plus / SAF shipment data; only specific, sourced
  numbers shown (Green Claims compliant).
- *"What if the number looks small?"* → translate to tangible units + show growth over time + the
  national aggregate across 36 branches.

# 🔗 HOW THEY CONNECT (pitch as one system)
- **Green Receipt** = the *individual felt moment* (and the data source)
- **Seller Badge** = *amplification* through the highest-leverage persona (SMEs → their buyers)
- **Impact Counter** = *collective pride*, fed by the receipts, closing the loop

One narrative for the judges: **Feel it → Share it → Belong to it.** Covers all 5 criteria, fits the
constraints, and demonstrates Green-Claims literacy throughout (the thing that separates winners).

> Pitch options: lead with Green Receipt as the hero and present Badge + Counter as the "system that
> makes it spread and stick," OR pick one if the format wants a single tight idea (Green Receipt is the
> safest standalone — strongest all-rounder).
