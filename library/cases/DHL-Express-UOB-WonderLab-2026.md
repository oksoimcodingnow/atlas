## 🎯 THE ONE THING (core problem)
DHL ServicePoint customers are **very satisfied (83%)** — but only about *service, helpfulness, professionalism*. They have **NO idea DHL does serious green-logistics work**. Sustainability **awareness is LOW**.

**KEY QUESTION (p16):**
> How do we make customers realize that every time they ship abroad at one of DHL's **36 ServicePoints**, they're helping reduce environmental impact alongside DHL?

➡️ This is an **awareness / communication** problem — the green stuff already exists, people just don't know.

## 🌱 What DHL actually does for the planet (proof points / ammo)
- **GoGreen Plus** ⭐ — flagship: customers cut shipping carbon via **Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF)**; available to walk-in ServicePoint customers. **This is the thing to communicate.**
- **EV fleet** — first logistics co. in Thailand to use EVs commercially. TH: **43 EVs (18.9%)**, goal 60% by 2030. E-bikes in BKK since 2020.
  - **Global: 50,604 e-vehicles = 46.2%** of DHL's worldwide fleet (p11)
- **SAF in cargo planes** — now **10%** of fuel
- **Solar / renewable buildings** — 13 Thai facilities (**92%**, goal 100% by 2030). **Global: 96.9%** of DHL buildings run on renewable energy (p11)
- Global: cut **2.1M metric tons** carbon in 2025
- **Net Zero target:** reduce GHG from **40M → under 29M metric tons by 2030**, then **Net Zero by 2050** (p8)
- Timeline of TH action: E-bikes (2020) → solar on facilities (2021) → 16 EVs commercial first-mover (2022) → GoGreen Plus + SAF launch (2023) → more EVs in the EEC eastern region (2024) (p10)

## ⚠️ THE TRAP — Green Claims rules (Appendix p17)
DHL is legally strict about green marketing. A winning team shows it understands this.
- ✅ **DO:** be specific & measurable ("% emissions cut"), explain *how* (SAF, insetting), plain language, transparent about scope
- ❌ **DON'T:** vague words alone (*"green," "eco-friendly," "sustainable"*), exaggerate, rely on offsetting alone
- ❌ **NEVER say:** *"emission-free," "climate neutral," "climate positive"*
- **Rule of thumb:** specific, transparent, evidence-backed — *if you can't prove it, don't say it.*
