## 🟡 USE WITH CARE (true but qualify, or easily mis-stated)

| Claim | Verdict | What to actually say |
|---|---|---|
| Thai WTP premium | 🟡 | Don't say "9.7% in Thailand." Correct: **APAC ~11%** (above the global 9.7%) — Thailand sits in APAC. Say "Thai/APAC consumers, ~11%, above the global average." [PwC APAC] |
| SAF cuts "up to 80%" CO₂ | 🟡 | True only for **waste feedstocks** (UCO ~76–97%); crop-based ~55%. Say **"up to ~80% on waste-based SAF,"** never a blanket 80%. [ICCT] |
| "SAF flew on your parcel" | 🟡→✅ | Never claim the parcel physically flew on SAF (book-and-claim). Say **"you funded a verified reduction via SAF in DHL's network."** This *is* your winning Green-Claims move. |
| €7B DHL climate investment | 🟡 | Real (DHL Group decarb roadmap, OpEx+CapEx to 2030) but it's **group-wide, not Thailand**. Say "DHL Group's €7B." [DHL Group] |
| "IATA: 10%-by-2030 unreachable" | 🟡 | Directionally right (IATA flags supply shortfalls; 2026 supply growth slowing) but don't over-quote a hard 10% target as fact. Say "industry bodies warn SAF supply is far behind targets." [IATA/S&P] |

## 🔴 FIX OR DROP (unsupported / shaky — don't say as-is)

| Claim | Verdict | Why + the fix |
|---|---|---|
| "**97%** of Thais concerned about climate" | 🔴 | Couldn't confirm 97% in the PwC source. **Drop it.** Use the supported Thai stat instead: **79% interested in EVs/HEVs** (PwC Thailand). |
| FedEx SAF "only at 2 US airports" | 🟡🔴 | Plausible but not cleanly verified in this pass. **Soften** to "FedEx's SAF use is limited / early-stage" rather than a specific airport count, unless you re-source it. |
| Any "66%" / "80% will pay more" | 🟡 | These come from different surveys (NielsenIQ 66%, PwC 80% "willing to pay more"). Don't blend them. Pick **one** (PwC: 80% willing, 9.7% premium) and cite it. |
