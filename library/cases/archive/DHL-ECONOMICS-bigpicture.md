## 6. NEW STRATEGIC PLAYS (ideas worth adding)

### ⭐ A. "Your Green Year" — a Spotify-Wrapped for green shipping
A year-end (or quarterly) **personalized, shareable impact summary**: *"In 2026 you shipped 48 times and cut
142 kg CO₂ — that's like X."* Digital via LINE/email, near-zero cost, **built to be screenshotted and posted.**
Perfect for SMEs to flex to *their* customers. Turns the Impact data into a **viral moment**, not just a counter.
→ Strongest new add: pairs the Receipt's data with the Wrapped phenomenon people already love to share.

### ⭐ B. The UOB co-marketing play (use the sponsor)
UOB runs a **Sustainability Compass for SMEs** and a huge SME banking base. **Partner the campaign with UOB's
own SME customers** — co-branded "ship greener, bank greener." Free reach through the sponsor's channels *and*
it tells the judges you see the whole ecosystem. (Low/no cost, high symbolic value.)

### C. "Green Streak" loyalty (drive the behaviour, not just awareness)
Track consecutive green shipments; small milestone nudges ("5 green ships in a row!"). Targets repeat SME
shippers → converts one-time awareness into a habit (the behaviour KPI).

### D. Launch "Match Moment" (seed adoption)
At launch, a partner (not the ฿5M) **matches the CO₂** on a customer's *first* GoGreen Plus shipment — lowers
the trial barrier, kick-starts the flywheel. Small, time-boxed, attention-grabbing.
