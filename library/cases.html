AspectJet fuelSAF

Why the premium matters: SAF costing 2–4× more is why GoGreen Plus has a surcharge — and why it's credible. Cheap = suspect; DHL paying ~$300/tonne for real cuts = trustworthy.

The carbon lifecycle — where the CO₂ actually goes

SAF emits roughly the same CO₂ at the tailpipe as jet fuel. The saving isn't in the burn — it's in where the carbon came from ("well-to-wake").
  • Fossil jet fuel = one-way street: ancient carbon dug up from underground → burned → added to the air. Atmosphere CO₂ ↑.
  • SAF = near closed loop: plants/waste absorbed CO₂ from today's air → become fuel → burned → carbon returned. Roughly circular.
  • Why ~50–80%, not zero: making & moving SAF still uses some energy — that's the leftover ~20–50%. Waste feedstocks (used cooking oil) score best.

Judge-line: "SAF doesn't burn cleaner — it recycles carbon. Fossil fuel adds ancient carbon to the sky; SAF burns carbon plants pulled from today's air, so the loop nearly closes."

💵 The money story (margin of selling eco)
Money thesis: our ฿5M doesn't pay for carbon — customers do (GoGreen Plus). The ฿5M buys awareness → awareness drives opt-ins → opt-ins are high-margin revenue for DHL AND a conversion lift for sellers. Green = a margin story, not a cost. (UOB is a bank — they reward this.)

Margin for DHL

  • GoGreen Plus ≈ €325/tonne CO₂e, reduction selectable 10–100% (1 click = 30%). Every opt-in = incremental revenue on a parcel that was shipping anyway → near-zero extra cost to sell.
  • Chain: ฿5M awareness → more customers know/feel good → opt-in rate ↑ (today low, because awareness is low — the case's whole problem) → high-margin green revenue + loyalty halo.
  • Premium moat: 9.7% stated willingness-to-pay premium (PwC 2024); a credible green reason-to-choose protects pricing power vs. cheaper couriers.
  • Honest caveat: stated WTP ≠ actual spend → that's why our frame is "you're already helping, free, just by shipping." Margin is upside, not a dependency.

Margin for the SME seller (why they spread the badge)

  • ESG claims track with faster growth: McKinsey × NielsenIQ ($400B, 600k SKUs, 5 yrs) — claim products grew 28% vs 20%, were 56% of all growth, multi-claim grew ~2×.
  • Eco-at-checkout lifts conversion +10–25% — cases: +22% (Nuzest), +19% (Tribe Kelley) — even when only ~1 in 10 buyers opt in. The lift comes from the signal of values, not everyone going green.
  • Marketplaces (Amazon/Etsy) run sustainability badges + may rank by them → a DHL-verified badge is a free sales tool sellers will use across channels.
  • Seller P&L: cost ≈ 0 (badge free, surcharge small/optional, often passed to buyer) · benefit = trust + conversion lift + faster-growth claim. → margin-positive credential = why the multiplier is self-interested, not charity.

The two-sided flywheel

฿5M awareness → customers opt in (DHL margin ↑) + sellers get badge (seller conversion +10–25%) → sellers broadcast it free → more green shipping → awareness compounds → margin compounds on both sides. Awareness is the only thing we buy; margin is what it manufactures.
