📋 Full case summary

The core problem

DHL ServicePoint customers are very satisfied (83%) — but only about service, helpfulness, professionalism. They have no idea DHL does serious green-logistics work. Awareness is low.

Key Question (p16): How do we make customers realize that every time they ship abroad at one of DHL's 36 ServicePoints, they're helping reduce environmental impact alongside DHL?

What DHL actually does for the planet (your ammo)

  • GoGreen Plus ⭐ — cut shipping carbon via SAF; available to walk-in customers. The thing to communicate.
  • EV fleet — first logistics co. in TH to use EVs commercially. TH: 43 EVs (18.9%), goal 60% by 2030.
  • SAF in cargo planes — now 10% of fuel.
  • Solar on 13 Thai facilities (92%, goal 100% by 2030).
  • Global: cut 2.1M metric tons carbon in 2025 · Net Zero by 2050.

Target personas (pick main + secondary)

  • Micro SMEs / online sellers — overseas orders (frequent shippers)
  • Expats — shipping home / fulfilling orders
  • Students — studying/moving abroad, or families sending to them
  • Tourists — short-term visitors shipping home

⚠️ The trap — Green Claims rules (Appendix p17)

DoDon't
Specific & measurable ("% emissions cut"); explain how (SAF, insetting); plain language; transparent scope Vague words alone ("green," "eco-friendly," "sustainable"); exaggerate; rely on offsetting alone; NEVER "emission-free," "climate neutral," "climate positive"

Rule of thumb: specific, transparent, evidence-backed — if you can't prove it, don't say it.

💡 Our idea: "Your Green Receipt"

The Big Idea — "Your Green Receipt" (ใบเสร็จสีเขียว)

Turn the receipt every shipper already gets into the proof that "you just helped the planet." The green already exists — what's missing is the moment the customer feels it.

The receipt (physical + digital via LINE/email) carries: "This shipment flew on SAF — you helped cut X kg CO₂," with a QR explaining how (keeps it Green-Claims-compliant).

Target: SME / online sellers (primary) + Students (secondary)

  • Frequent shippers → the receipt repeats → awareness actually builds (measurable)
  • Multiplier (B2B2C): the seller passes the green message to their own customers ("I ship greener with DHL" = their selling point)
  • Reachable in budget/time via LINE OA, seller groups, social commerce

Evolution hook: Green Receipt → a shareable badge for sellers, "This seller ships greener with DHL." Gives them value, not just info.

Why it wins the 5 criteria

  • Creativity: reframes a boring receipt into a thank-you moment
  • Insight: answers the Key Question at the exact point of action
  • Feasibility: existing receipts + screens + LINE; no new tech, no celebs
  • Impact/ROI: QR scans, LINE opens, before/after awareness survey
  • Storytelling: "the proof is in your hands"
