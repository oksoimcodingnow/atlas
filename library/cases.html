🏆 The idea — "Ship Love. Ship Light."
Big Idea — "Proof That Travels": we don't tell people DHL is green — we hand them proof they personally helped, and let it travel: onto the box, across the border, onto a national map, into the seller's shop.

🔬 Situation (read first with the team)
DHL already does the green work and customers already love DHL (83% satisfied) — they just don't know the two are connected. The green is invisible at the point of sale. Connect them at the moment of shipping, compliantly, and let the customers who ship most carry the message.

Key Question (p16): how do we make customers realize that every time they ship at one of DHL's 36 ServicePoints, they're helping the environment alongside DHL?

Problem tree: low awareness (symptom) → green invisible at point of sale (trunk) → no green message on receipt/screen/staff; GoGreen Plus not surfaced; customers come for speed; legal caution (roots).

💵 The money & the ฿5M budget
Money thesis: the ฿5M doesn't pay for carbon — customers do (GoGreen Plus). It buys awareness → opt-ins (high-margin for DHL) + seller conversion lift. OpEx, not CapEx — a 3-month marketing spend, no asset built. Judo line: "DHL already made the €7B CapEx; our ฿5M is awareness OpEx."

Why it pays

  • DHL: GoGreen Plus ≈ €325/tonne = incremental revenue on a parcel already shipping; 9.7% WTP premium = pricing moat.
  • SME seller: ESG-claim products grew 28% vs 20% (McKinsey/NielsenIQ); eco-at-checkout lifts conversion +10–25% even at ~1-in-10 opt-in. Badge = margin-positive credential → why sellers spread it.

✅ The proof — science, Green Claims & facts

SAF science (defend it)

SAF = renewable/waste jet fuel, drop-in (same speed). Costs 2–4×, under 1% of supply (→ why book-and-claim exists). Cuts up to ~80% lifecycle CO₂ on waste feedstocks. The saving is biogenic carbon (from today's air), not the tailpipe.

Green Claims — the winner-separator

  • Book-and-claim: say "you funded a verified reduction via SAF," NEVER "your parcel flew on SAF."
  • Cite GLEC Framework / ISO 14083 — the industry standard, and DHL is a member → citing the method DHL itself uses.
  • EmpCo binding 27 Sep 2026 bans generic + offset claims; DHL's insetting survives it.
  • Never: "green/eco-friendly/sustainable" alone, "emission-free," "climate neutral." Always: specific %, explain how, provable.

Fact audit — what to say out loud

🟢 Lead: 28% vs 20% ESG growth · +10–25% conversion · 43 vs 12 EVs (DHL vs FedEx) · €325/tonne · SAF 2–4×.
🟡 Qualify: WTP "APAC ~11% / global 9.7%" · SAF "up to ~80% on waste" · €7B = DHL Group.
🔴 Dropped: "97% concerned" (→ 79% EV-interested) · FedEx "2 US airports" (→ "limited").

The 3 to lead the pitch: 28% vs 20% · +10–25% conversion · 43 vs 12 EVs.

🎤 The pitch & creative angle

6-beat pitch flow

  1. Emotion — 20-sec story: a parcel sent abroad to someone loved.
  2. Gap — 83% love DHL; nobody knows it's green.
  3. Idea — "Proof That Travels" / "Ship Love. Ship Light."
  4. System — Feel→Carry→Belong→Spread (+ the tier closes the loop).
  5. Proof — precedents + reuses DHL's real assets + GLEC/book-and-claim.
  6. Close — Green-Claims mastery + the UOB mirror + "X tonnes cut, a country that knows."

Creative angle

International is the gift — the parcel crosses borders, so the green story can too. Emotional Thai layer + SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities): shipping is an equalizer — even a small home seller reaches the world. Carbon = the proof; opportunity = the heart. Precedents to borrow: Patagonia (transparency), EcoCart (impact counters), Maersk (dashboards), TOMS (participation).
