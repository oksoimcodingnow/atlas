Challenge #1 · "Fast to the World. Kind to the Planet."
Active
🔬 Situation analysis (read first with the team)
Situation in one line: DHL already does the green work and customers already love DHL — they just don't know the two are connected. Connect them at the moment of shipping, compliantly, and let the customers who ship most carry the message.

5C snapshot

  • Company: market leader, real first-mover green creds (EVs, SAF, solar, GoGreen Plus). Has done the work, hasn't communicated it at the counter.
  • Customers: come for speed/reliability, not green. 83% satisfied — on service, not sustainability. SMEs = highest value (repeat + own audience).
  • Competitors: rivals also green but communicate it poorly too. Real competitor = indifference/attention at the counter, not another courier.
  • Collaborators (no celebs): SME sellers as broadcasters · staff · seller communities · universities · owned channels (LINE, FB/IG, branch screens).
  • Context: tightening green-marketing law (specificity required) + rising eco-awareness + green skepticism (vague claims distrusted).

The problem tree

Symptom: low awareness of DHL's sustainability → Trunk: the green is real but invisible at the moment of shipping → Roots: (1) no green message at point of sale, (2) GoGreen Plus not surfaced to walk-ins, (3) customers come for speed, (4) legal caution → under-communication.

The awareness funnel (where we lose people)

Aware DHL is green? ✗ low → Understand THEY're part of it? ✗ lower → Feel something? ✗ (no moment exists) → Act again / tell others? ✗ (no loop). Intervene: felt moment (Green Receipt) · tell-others loop (Seller Badge) · belonging (Impact Counter).

Key tensions for the team

  • Speed vs. message (don't slow the counter → tiny passive touchpoints)
  • Awareness vs. behavior (decide what "success" means → sets KPIs)
  • Specific vs. emotional (pair a hard number with a human framing)
  • One persona vs. all four (recommend SME primary, students secondary)
  • Compliance vs. boldness (bold format, conservative claims)

So what (what the situation demands)

A winning idea must: live at the point of sale · make the customer a participant · use a specific provable number in a human moment · exploit the SME multiplier · fit ฿5M/36/3mo, no celebs · visibly respect Green Claims. → This is exactly why the Green Receipt + Seller Badge + Impact Counter spine fits: it's the situation's logical conclusion.

📋 Full case summary

The core problem

DHL ServicePoint customers are very satisfied (83%) — but only about service, helpfulness, professionalism. They have no idea DHL does serious green-logistics work. Awareness is low.

Key Question (p16): How do we make customers realize that every time they ship abroad at one of DHL's 36 ServicePoints, they're helping reduce environmental impact alongside DHL?

What DHL actually does for the planet (your ammo)

  • GoGreen Plus ⭐ — cut shipping carbon via SAF; available to walk-in customers. The thing to communicate.
  • EV fleet — first logistics co. in TH to use EVs commercially. TH: 43 EVs (18.9%); global 50,604 EVs = 46.2% of fleet. Goal 60% by 2030.
  • SAF in cargo planes — now 10% of fuel.
  • Solar / renewable buildings — 13 Thai facilities (92%); global 96.9% renewable. Goal 100% by 2030.
  • Global: cut 2.1M metric tons carbon in 2025.
  • Net Zero target: 40M → under 29M metric tons GHG by 2030, then Net Zero by 2050.

Target personas (pick main + secondary)

  • Micro SMEs / online sellers — overseas orders (frequent shippers)
  • Expats — shipping home / fulfilling orders
  • Students — studying/moving abroad, or families sending to them
  • Tourists — short-term visitors shipping home

⚠️ The trap — Green Claims rules (Appendix p17)

DoDon't
Specific & measurable ("% emissions cut"); explain how (SAF, insetting); plain language; transparent scope Vague words alone ("green," "eco-friendly," "sustainable"); exaggerate; rely on offsetting alone; NEVER "emission-free," "climate neutral," "climate positive"

Rule of thumb: specific, transparent, evidence-backed — if you can't prove it, don't say it.

💡 Our idea: "Your Green Receipt"

The Big Idea — "Your Green Receipt" (ใบเสร็จสีเขียว)

Turn the receipt every shipper already gets into the proof that "you just helped the planet." The green already exists — what's missing is the moment the customer feels it.

The receipt (physical + digital via LINE/email) carries: "This shipment flew on SAF — you helped cut X kg CO₂," with a QR explaining how (keeps it Green-Claims-compliant).

Target: SME / online sellers (primary) + Students (secondary)

  • Frequent shippers → the receipt repeats → awareness actually builds (measurable)
  • Multiplier (B2B2C): the seller passes the green message to their own customers ("I ship greener with DHL" = their selling point)
  • Reachable in budget/time via LINE OA, seller groups, social commerce

Evolution hook: Green Receipt → a shareable badge for sellers, "This seller ships greener with DHL." Gives them value, not just info.

Why it wins the 5 criteria

  • Creativity: reframes a boring receipt into a thank-you moment
  • Insight: answers the Key Question at the exact point of action
  • Feasibility: existing receipts + screens + LINE; no new tech, no celebs
  • Impact/ROI: QR scans, LINE opens, before/after awareness survey
  • Storytelling: "the proof is in your hands"

🏆 Top 3 ideas — deep dive
One system: DHL's green is real but invisible. We make it felt (Green Receipt), spread it through frequent shippers (Seller Badge), and make it collective (Impact Counter). Feel → Share → Belong.

⭐ 1. Your Green Receipt — the felt moment

Slogan: "Proof you helped the planet." / "ส่งของวันนี้ ช่วยโลกไปด้วยกัน"

Mechanic: receipt (paper + LINE/email) shows "you helped cut X kg CO₂" + QR explaining how (SAF/insetting) + carbon-as-tangible ("≈ X km not driven") + a running personal total.

  • Journey: staff one-liner → ship → Green Receipt → scan QR → keep/share → return, total grows
  • KPIs: QR scans · LINE opens · awareness lift (pre/post survey) · GoGreen Plus uptake · repeat rate
  • Tough Q: "receipt gets tossed" → it's also digital + on-screen + staff line; "credible?" → QR shows method, never "carbon neutral"

⭐ 2. "Ships Greener with DHL" Seller Badge — the multiplier

Slogan: "Sell it. Ship it greener." / "ร้านนี้ส่งของแบบรักษ์โลกกับ DHL"

Mechanic: SME ships green → unlocks a verified badge in LINE OA → adds it to shop/socials/packaging → their buyers see it. One DHL message reaches the seller AND their whole audience (B2B2C earned reach).

  • Why it's the ROI play: SMEs ship most + have their own audiences + actually want the badge (it helps them sell)
  • KPIs: badge claims · seller-audience reach (earned) · repeat volume/seller · new customers attributed
  • Tough Q: "will sellers bother?" → it's a sales asset, 1-tap; "overclaim risk?" → wording controlled, scoped to green shipments only

⭐ 3. The Impact Counter — collective pride

Slogan: "Look what we cut, together." / "รวมกันเราลด CO₂ ได้เท่านี้"

Mechanic: live tally on each branch screen + online dashboard: "customers here cut ___ kg CO₂ this month," fed by the Green Receipt data → the three ideas plug together. Carbon shown as tangible units; national total across 36 branches.

  • Solves: "my one parcel feels tiny" → shared growing total people want to join
  • KPIs: screen dwell · dashboard visits · milestone shares · repeat correlation
  • Tough Q: "data source?" → real GoGreen Plus data, sourced numbers only; "looks small?" → tangible units + growth + national aggregate

How to pitch

Lead with Green Receipt as the hero; present Badge + Counter as "the system that makes it spread and stick." Or pitch Green Receipt alone if the format wants one tight idea (safest standalone). Narrative: Feel → Share → Belong — covers all 5 criteria + shows Green-Claims literacy (the winner-separator).
