Emergence · three simple rules

A thousand birds, no leader.

Every dot follows the same three local rules and talks to no one in charge. Yet a flock appears, splits, swirls, and reforms. Move your cursor through them. This is emergence, the same way a market is just a crowd with no boss.

Separation

Don't crowd your neighbours. Steer away from anyone too close.

Alignment

Go with the flow. Match the average heading of nearby birds.

Cohesion

Stay together. Drift toward the centre of your local group.

What you're watching: there is no master plan and no leader. Each bird only sees its neighbours and obeys those three rules. The flock, the splits, the swirling, all of it is emergent, structure that nobody designed appearing from simple parts acting locally. Turn a slider to zero and watch the behaviour collapse: no separation and they clump into a blob; no alignment and the flock dissolves. Markets work the same way, no one is in charge, yet trends and crashes emerge from a crowd of small decisions.
