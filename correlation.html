How to read this honestly. Correlation is what happened in the past — it does not predict the future, and most strong correlations are coincidence (the more pairs you look at, the more fake ones you'll find by chance). It is a tool to question your portfolio (am I really diversified?), not a trading signal. Markets also "correlate to 1 in a crash" — the diversification you see here can vanish exactly when you need it. Data is daily closes, delayed/illustrative. Not financial advice.
