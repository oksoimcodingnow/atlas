Can you beat the market?

Fifteen seconds. Tap to jump in and out of the market. Be long when it rises, flat when it dips, and finish ahead of just holding. Sounds easy. It is not.

Tap the chart, press Space, or use the button. Each trade costs a small fee.
The honest twist: every time you trade you pay a fee (the spread), and the price is mostly random. So even with perfect-feeling timing, the costs quietly bleed you, and the market drifts up whether you are clever or not. Most players finish behind simply holding. That is Lesson 9 and Luck vs Skill, turned into a game. Trade less, win more.
