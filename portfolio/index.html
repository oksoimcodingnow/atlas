The idea

From a strong theme to a real portfolio

We started from a wellness-tourism theme — a genuinely strong, coherent idea. The one thing any single theme can't do on its own is spread risk, because the stocks tend to move together.

So we built on it: keep a wellness anchor for the story, then add stocks from other sectors — a theme with a reason, diversified on purpose.

Method · Correlation matrix

Do the stocks move together or apart?

A correlation matrix is a table of how much each pair of stocks moves together, from −1 (opposite) to +1 (lockstep). Low correlation = real diversification — when some zig, others zag, and the bumps cancel out.
