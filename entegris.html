EntegrisENTG

A full valuation of the Spotlight's featured pick — moat, three scenarios, a reverse DCF, and an honest verdict. The conclusion isn't "it's cheap." It's "here's exactly what you'd be betting on, and how to judge it at today's price."

All financial inputs are estimates (~2024–25) and must be verified. My data has a cutoff. The method and the reverse-DCF tool are real and stay valid — just plug in today's actual price (and check the FCF/debt figures).

1 The business

Entegris makes the ultra-pure materials, chemicals, and filtration that flow through semiconductor fabs — the consumables used on every wafer. As chips get more advanced (more layers, smaller nodes, advanced packaging for AI/HBM), each wafer needs more of this content. So revenue rides the whole semiconductor ramp, not any one chip winner. It's a "toll on every wafer." The catch: it bought CMC Materials in 2022, taking on heavy debt that still shapes the equity story.

2 Moat — is the advantage durable?

Switching costsStrong
Materials are qualified into each customer's exact process recipe. Switching means re-qualifying — months of work and yield risk. This is the dominant moat.
Cost advantageModerate
Scale in ultra-high-purity manufacturing is genuinely hard to replicate cheaply.
IntangiblesModerate
Decades of materials know-how, patents, and deep customer co-development.
Network / scaleWeak
No network effects; some niches have few credible suppliers (partial efficient scale).

Verdict: narrow-to-wide moat, ~10–20yr durability, anchored by switching costs. Industry structure is favorable (high qualification barriers keep new entrants out; purity has no substitute). Historically ROIC > WACC (value-creating) — though the CMC debt has compressed returns lately. A genuinely high-quality business. The question was never quality — it's price.

6 Risks

Leverage. ~$4B net debt from CMC. Magnifies the downside; deleveraging pace is the swing factor.
Cyclicality. Semis boom and bust; FCF can stay depressed longer than you'd like.
China. Export controls can hit a chunk of sales.
Integration / M&A. Serial acquisitions can mask organic softness — watch organic growth + cash flow vs earnings.

7 The verdict

High-quality business, NOT an obvious bargain. The moat is real (switching costs) and the AI-driven secular demand is real. But on base-case fundamentals it's worth ~$35, the bear case (~$9) is brutal because of the debt, and the bull (~$70) needs a strong upcycle. So Entegris is a recovery + deleveraging bet on a quality compounder — not a hidden cheap gem. Use the reverse DCF at today's price: if the implied growth looks easy, the quality may be on sale; if it's demanding, you're paying up for a recovery that has to show up. Either way: size small for the leverage, and demand a margin of safety.
