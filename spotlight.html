Featured — the quiet toll-taker

⌖ low profile · real moat
ENTG · Nasdaq
Entegris
Makes the ultra-pure materials, chemicals, and filtration used to manufacture chips — the consumables that flow through every fab, on every wafer.
Under-the-radarhigh
Why it has potential
Everyone watches the chip designers and the equipment makers. Almost nobody watches the company selling the purity every fab consumes. Two things make it special: (1) it's a consumable, not a one-off tool — recurring revenue that grows every time a wafer is made, so it's less boom-bust than the equipment names; and (2) as chips get more complex (more layers, advanced packaging, AI nodes), each wafer needs more of Entegris's materials and filtration. It's a quiet toll on the entire semiconductor ramp, and it's deeply qualified into customers' recipes — switching means re-certifying a process, so the revenue is sticky.
The real risk
Still cyclical with the chip industry; carries debt from a big acquisition (CMC Materials); and has China export-control exposure that can hit sales. And like everything in the stack, it leans on the AI-capex wave continuing.
Before you act: check the current P/E and EV/EBITDA vs its own history and vs the equipment names (AMAT, LRCX); confirm the debt is coming down and free cash flow is healthy. Cheap relative to that recurring, sticky revenue = the thesis. Verify it.
