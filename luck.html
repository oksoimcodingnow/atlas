Survivorship & selection bias

How to spot a fake genius.

A hundred traders. None of them have any skill at all, every move is a literal coin flip. Run a year and one of them will look like a star. Then bet on that star and watch the magic vanish. This is the most expensive illusion in markets.

Hit Run a year. A hundred coin-flippers will fan out by pure luck, and the luckiest will look like a market wizard.
What you're watching: every trader here is identical and has zero skill, each step is a 50/50 coin flip. Yet after a year their paths fan out, and someone always ends up on top with a gorgeous track record. That track record is pure noise. When you "bet on the star" and run year two, the gold line drops back into the crowd, because there was never anything to persist. This is survivorship bias (you only notice the winners) and selection bias (the more people you watch, the more impressive the luckiest one looks). It is why a fund's past returns tell you almost nothing, and why Lesson 7 and Lesson 12 beg you to discount them.
